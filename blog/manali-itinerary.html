Manali Itinerary 2026 — 3, 4 & 5 Day Plans

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 8 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Planning a Manali trip and wondering how many days you need? 3 days is the minimum for a quick getaway. 4 days is ideal for a relaxed trip covering all major spots. 5 days lets you explore deeply — Rohtang Pass, Naggar Castle, and hidden gems. This guide gives you day-by-day itineraries with costs, timings, and tips for each duration.

🗓️ 3-Day Manali Itinerary (Quick Getaway)

Best for: Weekend trip from Delhi/Chandigarh. Covers highlights. Fast-paced.

Day 1
Arrival + Mall Road & Old Manali
Reach Manali by morning (overnight bus from Delhi). Check into hotel. Freshen up. Afternoon: Walk to Hadimba Temple (2 km, 20 min through cedar forest). Evening: Mall Road — shopping, dinner at a nice restaurant. Walk to Old Manali for the cafe vibe. Try Johnson's Cafe or The Lazy Dog.
Day 2
Solang Valley — Adventure Day
Start early (8 AM). Shared taxi to Solang (₹100-150, 45 min). Activities: Paragliding (₹2,500-3,500), cable car (₹500-700), zorbing (₹400-600). Lunch at Solang dhabas (₹150-250). Return by 4 PM. Evening: Vashisht hot springs (3 km, shared auto ₹50). Soak in natural sulphur springs. Dinner in Old Manali.
Day 3
Jogini Waterfall + Departure
Morning: Easy 2-3 km trek to Jogini Waterfall from Vashisht. Takes 2-3 hours round trip. Beautiful apple orchards on the way. Return by noon. Afternoon: Last lunch in Manali. Buy souvenirs on Mall Road. Board evening Volvo back to Delhi.

🗓️ 4-Day Manali Itinerary (Recommended)

Best for: Most travelers. Covers everything at a relaxed pace. Includes Rohtang if open.

Day 1
Arrival + Hadimba Temple + Old Manali
Same as 3-day plan Day 1. Settle in, explore Hadimba, Mall Road, Old Manali cafes. Relaxed pace — you have 4 days.
Day 2
Solang Valley — Full Day Adventure
Full day at Solang. All activities. Pack a picnic or eat at valley restaurants. Try the cable car for panoramic views. Return by 5 PM. Evening at leisure — explore more cafes or rest at hotel.
Day 3
Rohtang Pass (Jun-Oct) OR Naggar Castle + Vashisht
If Rohtang is open (Jun-Oct): Start at 5 AM. Shared taxi (₹500-800). Experience snow at 3,978m. Return by 2 PM. If Rohtang is closed (Nov-May): Morning at Naggar Castle (20 km, ₹200-300 shared taxi). Afternoon at Vashisht hot springs. Evening: Old Manali.
Day 4
Jogini Waterfall + Departure
Morning trek to Jogini Waterfall. Return, have lunch, board evening bus. A perfect 4-day trip.

🗓️ 5-Day Manali Itinerary (Explorer)

Best for: Deep exploration. Includes offbeat places and relaxed pacing.

Day 1-3
Same as 4-Day Plan (Days 1-3)
Follow the 4-day plan for the first three days. Covers Hadimba, Solang, Rohtang/Naggar.
Day 4
Jogini Waterfall + Vashisht + Cafe Hopping
Morning: Trek to Jogini Waterfall (start early, beat the crowd). Afternoon: Vashisht hot springs. Evening: Dedicated Old Manali cafe hopping — Cafe 1947, Rasta Cafe, Drifters' Inn. Try trout fish, wood-fired pizza, and local brews.
Day 5
Naggar Castle + Art Gallery + Departure
Morning: Naggar Castle (sunrise views are stunning). Visit Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery nearby. Afternoon: Last meal in Manali. Souvenir shopping. Board evening Volvo. A relaxed, complete 5-day experience.

⚠️ Tips for Any Manali Itinerary

  1. Start early every day: Mountain weather is best in the morning. Afternoons can bring clouds.
  2. Book Volvo tickets for return: Evening buses to Delhi fill fast, especially on Sundays. Book on RedBus 2-3 days ahead.
  3. Rohtang Pass needs a permit: Apply online or through your hotel. Only 1,200 vehicles/day allowed.
  4. Carry cash: Many small cafes and dhabas don't accept cards. ATMs are available on Mall Road.
  5. Acclimatize on Day 1: Manali is at 2,050m. Some people get mild headaches. Rest, drink water, avoid alcohol on the first day.
