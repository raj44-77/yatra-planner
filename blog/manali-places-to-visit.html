Best Places to Visit in Manali 2026

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Manali is not just a hill station — it's a complete Himalayan experience. From adrenaline-pumping adventure sports at Solang Valley to the spiritual calm of ancient wooden temples, from snow-covered Rohtang Pass to the hippie cafes of Old Manali. This guide covers 8 must-visit places with distances, costs, timings, and insider tips.

🏔️ 1. Solang Valley

Easy14 km₹100-150 shared taxi2,560m

Manali's premier adventure hub. Paragliding (₹2,500-3,500), zorbing (₹400-600), cable car (₹500-700), horse riding, and ATV rides. In winter (Dec-Mar), it transforms into a ski resort with equipment rentals and instructors. Even if you don't do activities, the valley views alone are worth the trip. Best time: Year-round (adventure Apr-Nov, snow Dec-Mar).

🏔️ 2. Rohtang Pass

Easy51 kmPermit Required3,978m

The gateway to Lahaul and Spiti. At nearly 4,000 meters, Rohtang offers breathtaking views of glaciers, peaks, and the Chandra River. Snow is present even in June. Permit required — apply online or through your hotel (₹50-100). Only 1,200 vehicles allowed per day. Open: June – October. Closed in winter due to heavy snow. Shared taxi: ₹500-800/person.

🏛️ 3. Hadimba Devi Temple

Easy2 kmFree Entry2,050m

Built in 1553, this unique wooden temple is dedicated to Goddess Hadimba from the Mahabharata. Surrounded by a peaceful cedar forest (Dhungri Van Vihar), the pagoda-style architecture is stunning. A pleasant 20-minute walk from Mall Road. Timings: 8 AM – 6 PM. Tip: Visit early morning for the best light and fewer crowds.

🛍️ 4. Mall Road

Easy0 kmFree Entry

The heart of Manali. Lined with shops selling woolen clothes, handicrafts, Tibetan artifacts, and local food. Great for evening walks, people-watching, and trying local snacks. The Van Vihar park at one end offers boating. Tip: Bargain at shops — prices are inflated for tourists. Best time: Evening after 5 PM when the whole road comes alive.

☕ 5. Old Manali

Easy2 kmFree Entry

Cross the bridge from Mall Road and enter a different world. Old Manali is the backpacker and hippie hub — laid-back cafes, bakeries, guesthouses, and live music. The Manu Temple here is worth visiting. Must-try cafes: Cafe 1947, The Lazy Dog, Drifters' Cafe. Tip: Try the trout fish — a Manali specialty. Budget travelers: stay here, not Mall Road.

♨️ 6. Vashisht Hot Springs

Easy3 kmFree Entry

Natural sulphur hot water springs in Vashisht Village, just 3 km from Manali. Separate bathing areas for men and women. The water temperature stays around 40-45°C — incredibly soothing after a long journey. An ancient Vashisht Temple sits right next to the springs. Tip: Go early morning (7-8 AM) to avoid crowds. Carry a towel.

💧 7. Jogini Waterfall

Easy7 kmFree Entry

A beautiful waterfall cascading from 30 meters, surrounded by pine forests and mountain views. The 2-3 km trek from Vashisht Village is easy and takes about an hour. The path passes through apple orchards and small streams. Tip: Start early morning. Carry water and snacks. Combine with Vashisht hot springs — both can be done in half a day.

🏘️ 8. Naggar Castle

Easy20 km₹30 Entry

A 500-year-old castle built by Raja Sidh Singh of Kullu, now converted into a heritage hotel. The architecture is a stunning mix of Himalayan and European styles. The Nicholas Roerich Art Gallery nearby displays paintings of the famous Russian artist who made Naggar his home. Tip: Visit for sunset — the view of the Kullu Valley from here is unforgettable. Shared taxi from Manali: ₹200-300.
