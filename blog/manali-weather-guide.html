📊 Manali Weather — Month by Month

❄️ January HEAVY SNOW
-5°C to 8°C
Peak winter. Heavy snow. Solang Valley is a ski paradise. Perfect for honeymooners. Hotel prices at peak.
❄️ February SNOW
-3°C to 10°C
Snow continues. Less crowded than January. Great skiing. Budget-friendly compared to Dec-Jan.
🌸 March TRANSITION
2°C to 15°C
Snow melting. Pleasant days. Snow visible on peaks but roads clear. Fewer tourists.
✅ April – May BEST
8°C to 25°C
Perfect weather! Adventure sports, clear skies, Rohtang opens late May. Peak season starts mid-May.
✅ June PEAK
12°C to 28°C
Warmest month. Escape plains heat! Rohtang fully open. Maximum crowds and prices.
🌧️ July – August MONSOON
15°C to 25°C
Rainy season. Lush green, waterfalls. Landslide risk on roads. Cheaper hotels, fewer tourists.
🍂 September – October BEST VALUE
8°C to 22°C
Post-monsoon magic. Clear skies, golden autumn. Best budget + weather combo.
❄️ November FIRST SNOW
0°C to 15°C
Winter begins. First snowfall late Nov at Solang. Very few tourists. Great hotel deals.
🎄 December SNOW + XMAS
-3°C to 10°C
Winter wonderland! Christmas/New Year peak. Skiing at Solang. Cozy cafes with bonfires.

❄️ Manali Snowfall Guide

  • December – February: Peak snow. Solang 3-5 feet, Manali town 1-2 feet. Skiing season.
  • March: Snow melting. Skiing possible early March at Solang.
  • April – June: No snow in town. Lingering snow on Rohtang peaks.
  • July – October: No snow. Green meadows at Solang.
  • November: First light snowfall at Solang usually late November.

👕 What to Wear in Manali — Season by Season

Winter (Dec–Feb)

  • Thermal innerwear (top + bottom)
  • Heavy down jacket or parka
  • Waterproof snow boots
  • Woolen cap, gloves, muffler, earmuffs
  • Sunglasses — snow blindness is real

Summer (Mar–Jun)

  • Light t-shirts for daytime (15-28°C)
  • Light jacket for evenings (8-12°C)
  • Comfortable walking shoes
  • Sunscreen (SPF 50+), sunglasses, sun hat

Monsoon (Jul–Aug)

  • Quick-dry clothing — avoid cotton
  • Raincoat or heavy poncho
  • Waterproof shoes with grip

Autumn (Sep–Nov)

  • Layers: T-shirt → Sweater → Light jacket
  • Warm jacket for November evenings
  • Woolen cap for November mornings

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the temperature in Manali right now?

Manali temperature varies by season. Summer days (March-June) are 15-28°C. Winter nights (Dec-Feb) drop to -5°C. Check the live weather page for current conditions.

Does it snow in Manali in December?

Yes! Manali receives snowfall from late December to February. Solang Valley becomes a ski resort with 3-5 feet of snow. Manali town gets 1-2 feet.

What is the best month to visit Manali?

March-June for pleasant weather and adventure. December-February for snow and honeymoon. September-October for budget travelers.

Is Manali open in winter?

Yes! Manali is a year-round destination. Winter is perfect for snow and skiing. Rohtang Pass closes but Solang Valley remains open.
