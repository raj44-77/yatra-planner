📊 What's Included in Each Plan

🏔️ Basic Plan (Budget Traveler / Backpacker)

  • HRTC ordinary bus or general train to Delhi/Chandigarh
  • HRTC bus from Delhi to Manali (₹700-1,000)
  • Hostel or budget guesthouse (₹400-800/night)
  • Dhaba meals — 2 times per day (₹300-400/day)
  • Self-explored sightseeing (walking/shared taxis)
  • Best for: Students, solo travelers, backpackers

🏡 Comfort Plan (Family / Couples)

  • Sleeper/3AC train to Delhi + Volvo AC bus to Manali (₹1,200-1,800)
  • 3-star hotel near Mall Road (₹1,500-3,000/night)
  • Cafe/restaurant meals (₹600-800/day)
  • Shared taxi for Solang Valley & local sightseeing
  • Best for: Families, couples, first-time visitors

✨ Premium Plan (Luxury / Honeymoon)

  • Flight to Delhi/Chandigarh + Private SUV to Manali (₹12,000-16,000)
  • 4-5 star resort with mountain views (₹5,000-10,000/night)
  • Fine dining — multi-cuisine restaurants (₹1,200-1,500/day)
  • Private cab for entire trip + adventure sports included
  • Best for: Honeymooners, luxury travelers, special occasions

💡 How to Save Money on Your Manali Trip

  1. Travel in September or March: Hotel prices are 20-40% cheaper than peak season (May-June, Dec-Jan).
  2. Book Volvo buses 1-2 weeks early: Last-minute Volvo tickets cost ₹500-800 more on RedBus.
  3. Stay in Old Manali: Hostels and guesthouses here are ₹300-800 vs ₹1,500+ at Mall Road hotels.
  4. Eat at dhabas, not cafes: Old Manali dhabas serve great food at ₹60-150 vs ₹300-500 at tourist cafes.
  5. Share taxis to Solang: ₹100-150/person shared vs ₹2,500 for a private cab.
  6. Avoid Christmas/New Year week: Hotel prices triple. First week of January or late February is cheaper with the same snow.
  7. Walk Mall Road and Hadimba: Both are free and within walking distance. No taxi needed.
