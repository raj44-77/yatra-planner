Jaipur to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🏰 From the Pink City: Jaipur is only 650 km from Kedarnath — one of the closest cities on Yatra after Delhi. Direct trains to Haridwar take 10-12 hours. The total trip can be done in 4-5 days on a very reasonable budget. If you're traveling from anywhere in Rajasthan — Ajmer, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Kota — Jaipur is your gateway to the Himalayas.

🚂 Jaipur to Haridwar Trains

Jaipur Junction (JP) has direct trains to Haridwar. The journey is a comfortable overnight ride through Rajasthan and into Uttarakhand.

Our recommendation: Take the evening Haridwar Express (Sleeper ₹300-450). Board after dinner, sleep through the night, and arrive in Haridwar by morning. It's the most time-efficient option — you don't lose a day to travel.

Which Station in Jaipur?

  • Jaipur Junction (JP): The main railway station. All Haridwar trains depart from here. Located in the heart of the city — well-connected by auto, cab, and Jaipur Metro.
  • If you're coming from other Rajasthan cities: Ajmer (2 hrs), Udaipur (6 hrs), Jodhpur (5 hrs), Kota (4 hrs) — all have trains to Jaipur. Reach Jaipur first, then board the Haridwar train.

What to Do Before Boarding in Jaipur

  • Eat well: Jaipur Junction has good food stalls. Try the famous "Jaipur ki Kachori" from Rawat Misthan Bhandar (near station) — a perfect travel snack.
  • Stock up: Buy packaged water, dry fruits, and snacks for the journey. Prices at the station are reasonable.
  • Waiting room: If you arrive early, Jaipur Junction has a clean waiting hall (free) and AC waiting room (₹30-50/hour).

🗓️ Sample 5-Day Itinerary from Jaipur

Day 1: Jaipur → Board Evening Train

Spend the morning packing. Board the evening Haridwar Express from Jaipur Junction. Have dinner before boarding or carry food. Overnight on train.

Day 2: Haridwar → Mountains

Arrive Haridwar by morning. Freshen up at station (paid bathrooms ₹10-20). Bus to Rishikesh (45 min), then catch a shared taxi or bus to Sonprayag. Reach by evening. Stay at Sonprayag if late (₹500-800).

Day 3: Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Early morning jeep to Gaurikund (₹60-80). Start the 16 km trek by 6-7 AM. Stop at Jungle Chatti (4 km, tea/Maggi), Bheembali (7 km, paratha), Rambara (10 km, lunch thali ₹150-200). Reach Kedarnath by evening. Attend evening aarti.

Day 4: Darshan → Return Journey

Wake up at 3:30 AM for the morning aarti at 4:30 AM — a divine experience. After darshan, trek down to Gaurikund (4-6 hours). Bus/taxi back to Haridwar. Board evening or late-night train to Jaipur.

Day 5: Arrive Jaipur

Morning arrival at Jaipur Junction. Home by breakfast.

🌤️ Best Time to Travel from Jaipur

  • May–June: Peak season. Jaipur is extremely hot (40-45°C) but Kedarnath is pleasant (10-20°C). The contrast is dramatic — carry a jacket even if you're sweating in Jaipur.
  • September–October: Best time from Rajasthan. Jaipur weather is pleasant, Kedarnath has clear skies, and crowds are thinner. Hotel prices drop 20-30%.
  • July–August: Avoid — monsoon affects both the train route and the mountain roads.

🏨 Where to Stay in Haridwar (If Breaking Journey)

Sometimes the train reaches Haridwar in the evening and mountain buses have stopped. In that case:

  • GMVN Haridwar: ₹800-1,500/night. Near the bus stand. Clean, government-run.
  • Hotel Ganga Lahari: ₹1,500-2,500/night. Better rooms, Ganga view.
  • Budget lodges near station: ₹400-800/night. Basic but adequate for a night's rest.

⚠️ Jaipur-Specific Tips

  1. Pack for two climates: Jaipur at 40°C and Kedarnath at 5°C require completely different clothing. Carry a warm jacket, thermal, and gloves — even if you can't imagine needing them in Rajasthan's heat.
  2. Book Sleeper class: The overnight train is much more comfortable in Sleeper or 3AC. Worth the extra ₹200-500.
  3. Carry Rajasthan's dry snacks: Gathiya, sev, mathri — traditional Rajasthani snacks travel well and are perfect for the trek.
  4. Join a group from Jaipur: Many travel agencies in Jaipur organize Kedarnath group tours. If you prefer company, this is a good option — but they charge ₹10,000-15,000 per person. Yatra's DIY plan saves you 30-50%.
  5. Connect from anywhere in Rajasthan: If you're in Ajmer, Udaipur, Jodhpur, Bikaner, or Kota — take a train to Jaipur first, then follow this guide. Jaipur is the best gateway from Rajasthan.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kedarnath from Jaipur?

Approximately 650 km. One of the shorter routes on Yatra.

Direct train from Jaipur to Kedarnath?

No. Nearest railhead is Haridwar. Direct trains from Jaipur Junction — no change needed.

Is it safe to travel from Jaipur alone?

Yes. Thousands of solo travelers from Rajasthan do this route every year. The path is well-marked and there are enough fellow pilgrims to never feel alone.
