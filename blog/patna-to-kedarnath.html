Patna to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 8 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Planning a Kedarnath Yatra from Patna? This guide covers everything — train options with exact numbers and timings, bus routes, total budget breakdown, where to eat, where to stay, and common mistakes to avoid. Let's get you to the Himalayas.

🚂 Patna to Haridwar Trains

The first leg of your journey is Patna Junction (PNBE) to Haridwar (HW) — approximately 1,100 km, 17-18 hours. Patna has a big advantage over cities like Bhagalpur: direct trains to Haridwar — no need to change at another station.

🚌 Haridwar to Kedarnath — The Mountain Route

From Haridwar, the journey continues through Rishikesh, Devprayag, Srinagar, Rudraprayag, Guptkashi, Sonprayag, and Gaurikund — before the final 16 km trek to the temple.

🏨 Where to Stay at Kedarnath

  • GMVN Guest House: Government-run, clean, ₹800-2,500/night. Best option for most travelers.
  • Private Lodges: ₹600-1,000/night. Basic but functional. Hot water may cost extra.
  • Premium Stays: ₹5,000-10,000/night. Kedar Valley Resort — attached bathroom, geyser, mountain views.

🍽️ Food Costs on the Route

  • On train: ₹80-120 for thali from pantry/vendors
  • At Srinagar/Rudraprayag: ₹100-200 for Garhwali thali at dhabas
  • On the trek: ₹50-80 for Maggi/tea at Jungle Chatti, ₹150-200 for thali at Rambara
  • At Kedarnath: ₹150-250 for dinner thali

🌤️ Best Time to Visit

  • May–June: Peak season, best weather, most crowded
  • September–October: Best value — clear skies, fewer people, 20-30% cheaper hotels
  • July–August: Avoid — monsoon, landslides, unpredictable road closures

⚠️ Common Mistakes to Avoid

  1. Not booking train tickets early: Sleeper/3AC sells out 2-3 weeks before peak season. Book on IRCTC as soon as your dates are confirmed.
  2. Carrying too much luggage: You'll trek 16 km. Pack light — one backpack per person.
  3. Not carrying cash: ATMs disappear after Srinagar. Carry ₹3,000-5,000 in cash.
  4. Trusting touts for helicopter tickets: Only book through IRCTC or the official portal. "VIP darshan" offers are scams.
  5. Starting the trek too late: Begin by 6-7 AM. Afternoon weather can change suddenly in the mountains.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kedarnath from Patna?

Approximately 1,200 km. The journey takes 3-4 days round trip by train + bus + trek.

Is there a direct train from Patna to Kedarnath?

No. The nearest railway station is Haridwar (HW). From there, buses and shared taxis take you to the mountains.

Can senior citizens do this trip?

Yes! Ponies (₹2,500 one way), palkis (₹4,500), and helicopters (₹7,500) are available for those who cannot trek the 16 km. The Comfort Plan on Yatra is designed for elderly travelers.

Is Kedarnath open in December?

No. The temple closes in October/November and reopens in April/May. The entire area is under snow in winter.
