Kolkata to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🌏 From the City of Joy: Kolkata is the farthest city on Yatra's network — approximately 1,600 km from Kedarnath. The train journey alone takes 28-32 hours. Plan for 5-6 days minimum. This guide covers the exact trains from Howrah, what to pack for a long journey, and how to manage the budget.

🚂 Kolkata (Howrah) to Haridwar Trains

All Kedarnath-bound trains from Kolkata depart from Howrah Junction (HWH). There are direct trains to Haridwar — no need to change at Delhi or anywhere else.

Our recommendation: Take the Kumbha Express (1:00 PM, 3AC ₹1,800-2,200). It's faster than Doon Express and arrives at Haridwar at 10:30 AM — perfect timing. For a 30+ hour journey, 3AC is worth every rupee. Book 1-2 months in advance — these trains fill up fast, especially during May-June.

Which Station in Kolkata?

  • Howrah Junction (HWH): Main station. All Haridwar trains depart from here. Take a cab or local train from anywhere in Kolkata to Howrah.
  • Sealdah (SDAH): Some connecting trains. Check your ticket — most Haridwar trains are from Howrah only.

🗓️ Sample 6-Day Itinerary from Kolkata

Day 1: Kolkata → Train

Board Kumbha Express (1:00 PM) from Howrah. Carry lunch and dinner. Settle in for the long journey.

Day 2: Full Day on Train

Train passes through Jharkhand, Bihar, UP. Enjoy the changing landscape. Read, sleep, make friends.

Day 3: Haridwar → Mountains

Reach Haridwar 10:30 AM. Freshen up at station (paid toilets/bathrooms, ₹10-20). Bus to Rishikesh, then shared taxi to Sonprayag. Reach by evening.

Day 4: Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Jeep to Gaurikund early morning. Trek 16 km. Reach Kedarnath by evening.

Day 5: Darshan → Return

Morning aarti. Trek down. Bus back to Haridwar. Board evening train.

Day 6: Arrive Kolkata

Train reaches Howrah. Home.

⚠️ Kolkata-Specific Tips

  1. Book 3AC for the long haul: 30+ hours in General class is brutal. If budget allows, 3AC is a game-changer — clean linen, charging points, less crowded.
  2. Carry food for 2 days: The train pantry runs out on long routes. Pack dry snacks, fruits, and instant noodles. Hot water is available on the train.
  3. Break the journey if needed: Some travelers stop at Delhi for a night (hotels from ₹800 near NDLS) and continue fresh next morning. Adds a day but reduces fatigue.
  4. Flight alternative: Direct flights from Kolkata to Dehradun (₹7,000-10,000 one way) can cut travel time to 4-5 hours. See the Premium Plan on Yatra.
  5. Weather preparation: You'll go from Kolkata's 35°C heat to Kedarnath's 5°C nights. Pack accordingly — carry a jacket even if you're sweating at Howrah station.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kedarnath from Kolkata?

Approximately 1,600 km. The train journey alone is 28-32 hours.

Is there a direct train from Kolkata to Kedarnath?

No. Nearest railhead is Haridwar. Direct trains from Howrah to Haridwar — no need to change anywhere.

How many days needed from Kolkata?

Minimum 5-6 days. 2 days travel each way + 1-2 days at Kedarnath. Plan for a week if possible.

Flight vs Train from Kolkata?

Flight (₹14,000-20,000 round trip) takes 4-5 hours. Train (₹700-4,400 round trip) takes 28-32 hours. Choose based on budget and time.
