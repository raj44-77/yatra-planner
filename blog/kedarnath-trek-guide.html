Kedarnath Trek Complete Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 9 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🥾 The Sacred Walk: The 16 km trek from Gaurikund to Kedarnath Temple is the heart of the pilgrimage. It's not technical — the path is a well-paved stone trail wide enough for 4-5 people. But the altitude gain from 6,500 ft to 11,755 ft makes it physically demanding. This guide walks you through every checkpoint, what to expect at each, and how to prepare.

📊 Trek Overview

Distance16 km (one way)
Altitude Gain1,982m (6,500 ft) → 3,583m (11,755 ft)
Time5-8 hours (up) · 4-6 hours (down)
Path TypeWell-paved stone path throughout. Cannot get lost.
DifficultyModerate — not technical, but physically demanding
Best TimeMay–June & September–October
CostFree (pony/palki/helicopter extra)

🗺️ Checkpoint-by-Checkpoint Guide

📍 0 km
Gaurikund — The Starting Point
Altitude: 1,982m (6,500 ft)
What's here: Registration check post (show your e-pass), shops for trekking sticks (₹50-100), raincoats, water bottles, dry fruits. Pony and palki booking counters. Gauri Kund hot spring — many pilgrims take a holy dip before starting.
Tip: Use the toilet here (₹10). Fill water bottles. Buy a wooden trekking stick — it'll save your knees on the descent.
📍 4 km · ~1.5 hrs
Jungle Chatti — First Rest Stop
Altitude: 2,300m (7,545 ft)
What's here: 4-5 small dhabas. Hot tea (₹20), Maggi (₹50-80), biscuits, packaged water (₹30). Basic toilets (₹5-10). Shaded area with stone benches.
Tip: Rest 10-15 minutes. Don't sit too long — muscles will stiffen. This section is relatively flat and shaded by trees.
📍 7 km · ~3 hrs
Bheembali — The Climb Gets Serious
Altitude: 2,600m (8,530 ft)
What's here: Larger dhabas with proper seating. Aloo paratha (₹60-80), tea, light meals. Toilets available.
Tip: Rest 20-30 minutes. Have a proper snack — the climb gets noticeably steeper after this. The valley views open up beautifully from here.
📍 10 km · ~4.5 hrs
Rambara — Halfway Point (Lunch Stop)
Altitude: 2,900m (9,514 ft)
What's here: Multiple dhabas — "Shiva Dhaba" and "Himalayan Cafe" are the best. Fresh thali (₹150-200), small temple, last reliable water refill point, BSNL mobile network available here.
Tip: This is the best lunch stop. Eat well, rest 30-40 minutes. Refill ALL water bottles — the next reliable water is at Lincholi.
📍 13 km · ~6 hrs
Lincholi — Final Stop Before the Temple
Altitude: 3,200m (10,498 ft)
What's here: A few dhabas, water point. Kedarnath temple becomes visible from here — a huge morale boost!
Tip: Rest 10 minutes, drink water. The last 3 km are the steepest. Push through — you're almost there. The sight of the temple will carry you forward.
🛕 16 km · 7-8 hrs
Kedarnath Temple — YOU MADE IT!
Altitude: 3,583m (11,755 ft)
Surrounded by Kedarnath Peak (6,940m) and Kedar Dome (6,831m). Temple, lodges, dhabas all within 300m. Find your stay, freshen up, and head to the temple for evening aarti. You completed the pilgrimage! 🕉️

⚠️ Trek Safety Tips

  1. Start early — by 6-7 AM. Afternoon weather in the mountains can change suddenly. Starting late means you might get caught in rain or fog.
  2. Walk slowly. Take breaks. This is not a race. Altitude sickness is real — symptoms include headache, nausea, dizziness. If you feel unwell, STOP, rest, drink water, and descend if symptoms worsen.
  3. Stay hydrated. Drink water at every checkpoint. Dehydration makes altitude sickness worse.
  4. Use a trekking stick. Buy one at Gaurikund (₹50-100). It reduces knee strain by 30-40%, especially on the descent.
  5. Wear proper shoes. Waterproof trekking shoes with good grip. Break them in BEFORE the trip — new shoes cause blisters.
  6. Mobile network: Only BSNL works after Rambara. Inform family before starting the trek that you'll be unreachable for 1-2 days.
  7. Weather changes fast. Carry a lightweight poncho even if the sky is clear in the morning. Mountain weather is unpredictable.
  8. Medical aid: Medical post at Kedarnath near the temple. For serious emergencies, helicopter evacuation is available — contact the helpline at 0135-255989.

🎒 What to Carry on the Trek

  • Essentials: Trekking stick, 2L water bottle, ORS packets, dry fruits/chocolates, power bank, torch.
  • Clothing: Warm jacket (even in summer — nights are cold), raincoat/poncho, cap, sunglasses.
  • Documents: Registration e-pass (phone or print), Aadhar card.
  • Cash: ₹2,000-3,000 in small notes. No ATMs beyond Srinagar.
  • Medicine: Pain reliever, band-aids, altitude sickness medicine (consult doctor for Diamox).
