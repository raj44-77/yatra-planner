Lucknow to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 5 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🍀 Lucky Lucknow: Lucknow is one of the best-positioned cities for Kedarnath. At just 750 km, the train to Haridwar takes only 10-12 hours — a comfortable overnight journey. Total trip can be done in 4-5 days at one of the lowest costs on Yatra.

🚂 Lucknow to Haridwar Trains

Multiple direct trains pass through Lucknow (LKO) on their way to Haridwar. You don't need to change anywhere.

⚠️ Lucknow-Specific Tips

  1. Board from Lucknow Charbagh (LKO): All Haridwar trains pass through the main Charbagh station. Well-connected by auto, metro, and bus.
  2. Overnight trains are best: Board at night, sleep, arrive Haridwar by morning/noon. Saves a day of travel.
  3. Carry Lucknowi food: Pack some famous Lucknowi biryani or kebabs for the train journey — much better than pantry food!
  4. Connect from nearby cities too: If you're in Kanpur, Allahabad, or Gorakhpur — reach Lucknow first, then take the Haridwar train.
