Manali to Spiti Valley Road Trip Guide 2026

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 10 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🏔️ One of India's Greatest Road Trips: The Manali to Spiti Valley road trip is legendary — 200 km of some of the most breathtaking and challenging terrain in the Himalayas. You cross two high passes (Rohtang at 3,978m and Kunzum at 4,590m), drive alongside the Chandra River, and enter a landscape that looks like the moon. This guide covers everything: the complete route, permits you absolutely need, budget, bike vs car, places to visit, and critical safety tips.

🗺️ The Route: Manali → Spiti Valley (Kaza)

Total Distance: ~200 km | Time: 8-10 hours (non-stop) | Best Time: June – September

📍 0 km
Manali (2,050m) — Starting Point
Fill fuel to FULL. No petrol pump for the next 200 km until Kaza. Last petrol pump: Manali Mall Road. Stock up on water, snacks, and basic medicines. Get your permits in order (see permit section below). Start by 5-6 AM.
📍 38 km
Rohtang Pass (3,978m) — Gateway to Lahaul
First high pass. Permit check post here — have your Rohtang permit ready (digital or printed). Snow on the pass even in June. Stop for photos — the view of glaciers and peaks is unforgettable. Small dhabas for chai and Maggi. Toilets available.
📍 60 km
Gramphu — Road Splits
Important junction. Left goes to Leh (via Keylong). RIGHT goes to Spiti/Kaza. Take the RIGHT fork. The road gets rougher from here — this is where the real Spiti adventure begins.
📍 80 km
Chhatru / Batal — Lunch Stop
Small settlement with basic dhabas. Last reliable food stop before Kaza. Paratha, dal-rice, chai available. Fill water bottles. The landscape transforms dramatically after this point — greenery disappears, mountains become bare and rugged.
📍 110 km
Kunzum Pass (4,590m) — Roof of the Journey
Highest point of the trip. Kunzum Mata Temple at the top — travelers stop to pray for a safe journey. Breathtaking 360° views of the Chandra-Bhaga range. Can be very cold even in summer — carry a jacket. Short photo stop (10-15 min) due to altitude.
📍 130 km
Chandratal Lake Detour (Optional)
14 km detour from the main road. One of the most beautiful high-altitude lakes in India — crescent-shaped, deep blue water at 4,300m. The 14 km road is rough — takes 1-1.5 hours each way. Only attempt if you have daylight and a sturdy vehicle. Camping available near the lake (₹500-1,500).
📍 160 km
Losar — First Village of Spiti
The first village after Kunzum Pass. A few homestays and basic dhabas. Altitude: 4,080m. Beautiful traditional Spitian houses. Good place to rest if you're tired — homestays available (₹500-1,000/night).
📍 200 km
🏁 Kaza (3,800m) — YOU MADE IT!
Spiti's main town. Petrol pump (finally!), ATMs (SBI works best), hotels, cafes, and the Spitian vibe. Base for exploring Key Monastery, Kibber, Langza, Hikkim, and Komic. You've completed one of India's most epic road trips!

🏔️ Must-Visit Places in Spiti Valley

  • Key Monastery (12 km from Kaza): 1,000-year-old monastery perched on a hilltop. Breathtaking views of the Spiti River. Entry: ₹50. Photography allowed outside.
  • Kibber Village (20 km): One of the highest inhabited villages in the world (4,270m). Spot snow leopards in winter. Homestays available.
  • Langza (15 km): Famous for the giant Buddha statue with Himalayan backdrop. Fossil-rich area — you can find marine fossils here.
  • Hikkim (16 km): Home to the world's highest post office (4,440m). Send a postcard home — it actually reaches!
  • Komic (18 km): One of the highest villages connected by road (4,587m). The Tangyud Monastery here is stunning.
  • Chandratal Lake: The crown jewel. Crescent-shaped lake at 4,300m. Best visited on the way to or from Manali.

⚠️ Critical Safety Tips

  1. Acclimatize before Kunzum Pass: Spend at least 1-2 days in Manali before attempting this trip. Altitude sickness at 4,500m is real — headache, nausea, dizziness. If symptoms are severe, descend immediately.
  2. Start early — by 5-6 AM: The road takes 8-10 hours. You don't want to be on Kunzum Pass after 4 PM — weather can turn suddenly.
  3. No petrol pump between Manali and Kaza: Fill your tank FULL in Manali. Carry 5-10 liters extra if your vehicle has low mileage.
  4. No mobile network for 90% of the journey: BSNL works patchy near Kaza. Inform family before leaving Manali that you'll be unreachable for 1-2 days.
  5. Road is CLOSED from October to May: Kunzum Pass is buried under snow. Don't attempt this trip outside June-September.
  6. Carry basic medicines: Diamox (for altitude), pain relievers, band-aids, ORS. No pharmacies between Manali and Kaza.
  7. Weather changes in minutes: Sunny morning can turn into snowfall by afternoon. Carry warm layers and rain gear even in June.
