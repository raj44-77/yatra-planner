Delhi to Manali Complete Road Trip Guide 2026

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

The Delhi to Manali road trip is one of India's most iconic journeys — 550 km from the capital to the Himalayas. You'll cross Chandigarh, drive through the Shivalik hills, follow the Beas River through Mandi and Kullu, and wake up to mountain views. This guide covers every way to travel: budget HRTC bus, comfortable Volvo, and private car with the best stops along the way.

🚌 Option 1: HRTC Bus (Budget — ₹700-1,000)

Boarding: ISBT Kashmere Gate, Platform 35-38. Metro: Kashmere Gate (Red/Yellow/Violet lines).
Departure: 5 PM – 10 PM (multiple buses daily).
Duration: 12-14 hours. Reaches Manali next morning 6-8 AM.
Type: Non-AC, pushback seats. Stops at Karnal, Chandigarh, Mandi, Kullu for tea/meals.
Ticket: ₹700-1,000/person. Buy at ISBT counter or online at hrtc.gov.in.

🚌 Option 2: Volvo AC Bus (Comfort — ₹1,200-1,800)

HRTC Volvo / Private Volvo: AC, semi-sleeper seats, charging points, blanket provided. Fewer stops.
Private operators: Laxmi Holidays, City Land Travels, Ram Dalal — ₹1,500-2,200. Slightly more comfortable.
Booking: redbus.in, hrtc.gov.in, paytm. Book 1-2 weeks ahead for peak season (May-June, Dec-Jan).

🚗 Option 3: Private Car (Premium — ₹10,000-14,000)

Vehicle: Innova/Fortuner. Door-to-door from Delhi to Manali hotel. 11-12 hours with stops wherever you want — Murthal for breakfast, Chandigarh for lunch, Mandi for chai. Best for families and groups.
Round trip: ₹20,000-25,000 (1-3 people). Book via Savaari, MakeMyTrip, or local Delhi operators.

⚠️ Delhi to Manali Travel Tips

  1. Board from ISBT Kashmere Gate, not Anand Vihar: Most Manali buses leave from Kashmere Gate. Confirm your boarding point on the ticket.
  2. Carry a light blanket: Even ordinary buses get cold at night, especially after Chandigarh.
  3. Book return tickets together: Manali to Delhi return buses fill fast on Sundays. Book round trip on RedBus.
  4. Mandi is the best breakfast stop: If you're on a Volvo, the Mandi stop has clean restrooms and good chai.
  5. First view of Himalayas: Between Kullu and Manali, around 6-7 AM, you'll see the first snow peaks. Have your camera ready!
