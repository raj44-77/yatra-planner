📊 What's Included in Each Plan

Basic Plan (Budget)

  • General class train tickets (round trip)
  • Public bus + shared jeep for mountain transfers
  • Dhaba meals — 2 times per day
  • 1 night lodge/tent stay at Kedarnath (₹600-1,000)
  • 16 km trek — free
  • Miscellaneous: water, tea, trekking stick, tips

Comfort Plan (Balanced)

  • Sleeper/3AC train tickets (round trip)
  • Shared taxi for mountain transfers
  • Better restaurant meals — 2 times per day
  • 1 night private room at Kedarnath (₹2,000-3,500)
  • 16 km trek with snack stops (₹500)
  • AC waiting rooms at stations, porter tips

Premium Plan (Luxury)

  • Flight or AC First Class train
  • Private SUV with dedicated driver (round trip)
  • Premium dining — multi-cuisine restaurants
  • 1 night premium resort at Kedarnath (₹8,000-10,000)
  • Optional: Helicopter to Kedarnath (₹7,500 one way)
  • 24/7 travel guardian, VIP darshan arrangements

💡 How to Save Money on Your Kedarnath Trip

  1. Travel in September: Hotel prices drop 20-30% compared to May-June peak season.
  2. Book train tickets 3-4 weeks early: Last-minute tickets cost more or sell out.
  3. Carry your own food for the train: Station food is cheaper than pantry cars.
  4. Share rooms: Hotels charge per room, not per person. Traveling as a group of 4 cuts stay costs significantly.
  5. Avoid helicopters unless necessary: They add ₹15,000+ to your budget. The trek is free and is the whole point of the pilgrimage!
