Bhagalpur to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj (from Bhagalpur)

🏠 From My Hometown: I built Yatra because my own family in Bhagalpur struggled to plan our Kedarnath trip. There's no direct train from Bhagalpur to Haridwar — you must go via Patna first. This guide covers the exact route my family took, with real prices and train numbers. If we could do it, you can too.

🗺️ The Route: Bhagalpur → Patna → Haridwar → Kedarnath

Bhagalpur is a smaller city in Bihar, and unlike Patna, there is no direct train to Haridwar. You'll first travel to Patna Junction, then catch a Haridwar-bound train from there. This adds a few hours to your journey but the connection is straightforward and well-timed.

Key timing tip: The Kumbha Express at 5:45 PM connects perfectly with the morning Intercity from Bhagalpur. You'll have 5 hours at Patna — enough time to eat, rest, and board comfortably. This is the exact connection my family used.

What to Do During the 5-Hour Wait at Patna Junction

  • Lunch: IRCTC Food Plaza on Platform 1. Thali ₹100-150. Clean, air-conditioned.
  • Temple visit: Mahavir Mandir is a 5-minute walk from the station. Famous temple, good for blessings before the yatra.
  • Rest: General waiting hall (free) or AC waiting room (₹30-50/hour). Both are on Platform 1.
  • Book tickets: If you haven't booked the Haridwar train yet, reservation counter is on Platform 1.

⚠️ Bhagalpur-Specific Tips

  1. Book both trains together: On IRCTC, book Bhagalpur→Patna and Patna→Haridwar in one session. Keep a 3-5 hour buffer at Patna for delays.
  2. Carry food from home: The Bhagalpur-Patna Intercity doesn't have a pantry car. Pack breakfast/snacks from Bhagalpur station or home.
  3. Patna waiting hall is safe: If traveling with family, the AC waiting room at Patna Junction (₹50/hour) is clean, safe, and has charging points.
  4. Alternative route: Some travelers take a bus from Bhagalpur to Patna (₹150-200, 5-6 hours) if train tickets are unavailable. ISBT Bhagalpur has regular buses.
  5. Mobile network: Airtel/Jio work fine till Gaurikund. Only BSNL works on the trek. Carry a BSNL SIM or inform family before starting the trek.

🗓️ Sample 5-Day Itinerary from Bhagalpur

Day 1: Bhagalpur → Patna → Haridwar (Train)

6:45 AM Intercity to Patna (₹100). Lunch at Patna. 5:45 PM Kumbha Express to Haridwar. Overnight on train.

Day 2: Haridwar → Mountains

Reach Haridwar 10:30 AM. Bus to Rishikesh (45 min). Catch shared taxi to Sonprayag. Reach by evening. Stay at Sonprayag if late.

Day 3: Sonprayag → Kedarnath

Jeep to Gaurikund. Trek 16 km to Kedarnath (5-8 hrs). Evening aarti. Stay at lodge/tent.

Day 4: Darshan → Return

Morning darshan. Trek down. Bus back to Haridwar. Board evening train.

Day 5: Arrive Home

Reach Patna by afternoon. Connecting train to Bhagalpur. Home by evening.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a direct train from Bhagalpur to Kedarnath?

No. The nearest railhead is Haridwar. You must travel Bhagalpur→Patna→Haridwar by train, then bus+trek to Kedarnath.

How long does the Bhagalpur to Kedarnath trip take?

Minimum 5 days round trip. Day 1-2: travel, Day 3: trek up, Day 4: darshan + return, Day 5: reach home.

Is the Patna waiting hall safe for families?

Yes. The AC waiting room at Patna Junction (₹50/hour) is clean, safe, and regularly patrolled by RPF. My own family used it.

What if I miss the Patna→Haridwar connection?

The Doon Express (11:25 PM) and Gangasutlej Express (11:30 PM) are late-night backups. Or stay overnight at Patna (hotels from ₹800 near station) and take next day's train.
