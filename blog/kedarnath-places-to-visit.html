Best Places to Visit in Kedarnath 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Kedarnath is not just about the temple. The surrounding Himalayan landscape holds ancient temples, glacial lakes, mountain villages, and trekking routes that most pilgrims miss. This guide covers 6 must-visit places near Kedarnath — from easy 10-minute walks to challenging high-altitude treks.

🛕 1. Kedarnath Temple

Easy0 kmFree Entry3,583m

The main destination. One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva, built in the 8th century by Adi Shankaracharya. The temple is made of massive stone slabs that have survived centuries of Himalayan weather. Morning Aarti at 4:30 AM is a must-attend. The lingam here is pyramid-shaped — unique among Jyotirlingas.

Tip: Reach by 4 AM for the aarti. Photography not allowed inside. Visit Bhairavnath Temple (500m behind) for the best mountain photos at sunrise.

🗿 2. Bhairavnath Temple

Easy0.5 kmFree Entry3,600m

Just 500 meters behind the main temple, this small temple is dedicated to Bhairav — the fierce guardian of Kedarnath. According to legend, Bhairavnath protects the valley when the main temple closes for winter. The real reason to visit: the view. From here, you get the most spectacular panoramic view of Kedarnath peak and the entire valley.

Tip: Go right after morning aarti. Sunrise (5:30-6:30 AM) from this spot is the best photo you'll take on the entire yatra.

🏔️ 3. Vasuki Tal

Hard8 kmFree Entry4,150m

A stunning glacial lake surrounded by snow-capped peaks, named after Vasuki — the serpent king associated with Lord Shiva. The water is crystal blue-green and reflects the mountains like a mirror. This is a serious high-altitude trek — you gain 500m+ from Kedarnath. The lake remains partially frozen until late June.

Only for experienced trekkers. 4-5 hours one way. No shops or water beyond Kedarnath. Start at 4 AM and carry everything. The reward is absolute solitude — most pilgrims skip this, so you'll likely have the lake to yourself.

💧 4. Gandhi Sarovar (Chorabari Tal)

Moderate3 kmFree Entry3,900m

A beautiful glacial lake just 3 km from the temple, renamed in 1948 when Mahatma Gandhi's ashes were immersed here. Much more accessible than Vasuki Tal — about 1.5-2 hours one way. You can see small icebergs floating in the lake even in June.

Tip: Best visited in the morning. Combine with Bhairavnath Temple — both can be done in a single morning before starting your return trek.

💍 5. Triyuginarayan Temple

Easy25 kmFree Entry1,980m

Believed to be the exact spot where Lord Shiva and Goddess Parvati were married. The temple has an eternal flame (akhand dhuni) that devotees believe has been burning since the divine wedding — through three cosmic ages. Located near Sonprayag, accessible by road — no trekking needed.

Tip: Visit on your return journey. Shared taxis from Sonprayag (₹300-400). A peaceful, less-crowded temple — perfect for couples and families.

♨️ 6. Gaurikund

Easy14 kmFree Entry1,982m

The starting point of the Kedarnath trek and a sacred site itself. According to mythology, Goddess Parvati meditated here to win Lord Shiva's love. The natural hot water spring (Gauri Kund) is the highlight — water at ~45°C flows into bathing pools. Many pilgrims take a holy dip before starting the trek.

Tip: Take a dip both before AND after the trek. The hot water soothes sore muscles. Carry a towel and change of clothes — the air is cold even when the water is hot!
