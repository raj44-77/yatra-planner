Manali Honeymoon Guide 2026

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Manali is India's most popular honeymoon destination after Shimla — and for good reason. Snow-covered mountains, cozy cafes with bonfires, luxury resorts with valley views, and adventure sports for couples. Whether you want a winter wonderland wedding or a summer adventure honeymoon, Manali delivers. This guide covers costs, best hotels, romantic experiences, and a perfect 5-day itinerary.

💑 Most Romantic Experiences in Manali

  • Candlelight dinner with mountain views: Johnson's Cafe, The Lazy Dog, and resort restaurants offer special candlelight setups (₹1,500-5,000 per couple). Book in advance.
  • Private bonfire under the stars: Most Old Manali cafes and resorts arrange private bonfires with music and snacks (₹1,000-2,000).
  • Snow walk hand-in-hand at Solang: Winter mornings at Solang Valley are pure magic — fresh snow, no crowds, perfect for couples.
  • Vashisht hot springs together: Natural sulphur springs at 40°C. Separate bathing but the experience is unique.
  • Jogini Waterfall trek: Easy 2-3 km trek through apple orchards to a beautiful waterfall. Perfect couple activity.
  • Old Manali cafe hopping: Spend an afternoon exploring cozy cafes — Cafe 1947, Drifters', Rasta Cafe.

🗓️ 5-Day Honeymoon Itinerary

Day 1: Arrival + Mall Road

Check into your hotel. Evening walk on Mall Road. Dinner at a nice restaurant. Rest after the journey.

Day 2: Hadimba Temple + Old Manali

Morning: Hadimba Temple (peaceful cedar forest). Afternoon: Old Manali cafes. Evening: Bonfire at your hotel or a cafe.

Day 3: Solang Valley Adventure

Full day at Solang. Paragliding together, snow activities, cable car. Pack a picnic or eat at the valley restaurants.

Day 4: Vashisht + Jogini Waterfall

Morning: Vashisht hot springs. Late morning: Trek to Jogini Waterfall. Evening: Candlelight dinner — the highlight of your honeymoon.

Day 5: Naggar Castle + Departure

Morning visit to Naggar Castle (sunrise views are spectacular). Afternoon: Head back. Take the evening Volvo to Delhi.

❄️ Best Time for Manali Honeymoon

  • December – February (Snow): Most romantic. Snow everywhere. Cozy cafes, bonfires. But: heavy woolens needed, Rohtang closed. Budget: Higher (peak season).
  • March – June (Pleasant): Perfect weather. All activities open. Rohtang accessible. Budget: Moderate. Best for outdoor couples.
  • September – November (Autumn): Golden landscapes. Fewer tourists. Budget-friendly. Great for peaceful honeymoons.
