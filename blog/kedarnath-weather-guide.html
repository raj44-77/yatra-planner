🌡️ Temperature Guide — What to Expect

  • Summer days (May-June): 15-20°C — pleasant for trekking in a light jacket
  • Summer nights: 5-10°C — thermals and heavy jacket needed
  • Monsoon (July-August): 10-18°C — wet, humid, rain gear essential
  • Autumn (September-October): 2-18°C — beautiful days, freezing nights
  • Winter (November-April): -15°C to 5°C — temple closed, area inaccessible

❄️ Snowfall Guide

  • April–Early May: Lingering winter snow on the trek path. Some icy sections.
  • Late May–June: Snow melts from the trail. Clear path.
  • July–August: No snow. Rain instead.
  • September–October: First snowfall usually late October. Early snow possible.
  • November–March: Heavy snowfall. Temple buried under 3-6 feet.

🌧️ Monsoon Guide — Is Kedarnath Safe in July-August?

The monsoon hits Kedarnath from late June through August. Key concerns:

  • Landslide risk: The Rishikesh-Sonprayag road is vulnerable. You may get stranded.
  • Slippery trek: The 16 km stone path gets wet and mossy. Good grip shoes essential.
  • Cloudbursts: Rare but devastating (2013 disaster was caused by one).
  • Helicopters grounded: Don't rely on them during monsoon.

Recommendation: Avoid July and August. If unavoidable, check weather daily, start trek early, carry full rain gear.

👕 What to Wear in Kedarnath

May–June (Summer)

  • Day: Light t-shirt, sun hat, sunglasses, sunscreen (SPF 50+)
  • Night: Thermal, fleece, heavy down jacket (5°C)
  • Footwear: Waterproof trekking shoes with grip

July–August (Monsoon)

  • Full rain suit or heavy-duty poncho
  • Quick-dry clothing — avoid cotton
  • Waterproof backpack cover
  • Extra socks in sealed plastic bag

September–October (Autumn)

  • Layers: Thermal → T-shirt → Fleece → Jacket
  • Heavy jacket for nights (below 5°C by October)
  • Woolen cap and gloves for morning aarti

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What is the temperature in Kedarnath right now?

Kedarnath temperature varies by season. In summer (May-June) days are 15-20°C and nights 5-10°C. Check the live weather page for the current temperature and 10-day forecast.

Does it snow in Kedarnath in May?

Early May may have residual snow on the trek path. By late May, the trail is usually clear. Fresh snowfall in May is rare but possible at higher elevations.

What is the best month to visit Kedarnath?

May, June, and September are the best months. September offers clear skies, fewer crowds, and cheaper hotels. Avoid July-August (monsoon).

Is Kedarnath safe during monsoon?

July and August are risky due to landslides and slippery trek paths. If traveling during monsoon, check daily weather updates, start trekking early, and carry full rain gear.
