Varanasi to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🕉️ Kashi to Kedarnath: This is a journey between two of Lord Shiva's most sacred abodes — Varanasi (Kashi Vishwanath) and Kedarnath (the Jyotirlinga in the Himalayas). Many pilgrims complete Kashi darshan first, then head to Kedarnath. At ~1,000 km, direct trains take 18-20 hours. Plan for 5-6 days minimum.

🚂 Varanasi to Haridwar Trains

Varanasi Junction (BSB), also known as Varanasi Cantt, has direct trains to Haridwar. No need to change at any station.

Our recommendation: Take the Doon Express (8:30 AM, Sleeper ₹450). Board in the morning, travel through the day across UP, and reach Haridwar by early next morning. Book 3-4 weeks ahead on IRCTC.

Which Station in Varanasi?

  • Varanasi Junction / Cantt (BSB): The main station. All Haridwar trains depart from here. Located in the city center — easily accessible from Assi Ghat, Godowlia, and Dashashwamedh Ghat.
  • Manduadih (MUV): Some trains also stop here. Check your ticket — most Haridwar trains are from BSB.

Before You Leave Varanasi

  • Get Kashi Vishwanath darshan first: Many pilgrims visit Kashi Vishwanath Temple before heading to Kedarnath. It's considered auspicious to complete both Shiva darshans in one trip.
  • Carry Banarasi snacks: Kachori-sabzi, jalebi, and namkeen from Varanasi's famous shops travel well on the train.
  • Buy Gangajal: If you're visiting Kashi Vishwanath, carry a small bottle of Gangajal to offer at Kedarnath. It's a beautiful tradition.

⚠️ Varanasi-Specific Tips

  1. Combine Kashi Vishwanath + Kedarnath: Many pilgrims do both in one trip. Visit Kashi Vishwanath in the morning, board the Doon Express by 8:30 AM — perfect same-day plan.
  2. Gangajal offering: Carry Gangajal from Varanasi to offer at Kedarnath. It's a meaningful tradition connecting the two sacred sites.
  3. Travel light from Varanasi: You'll trek 16 km. Leave heavy luggage at Varanasi station's cloakroom (₹20-30/day) if you're returning.
  4. Varanasi to Prayagraj connection: If trains from Varanasi are full, take a 2-hour train to Prayagraj (Allahabad) — more Haridwar-bound trains depart from there.
