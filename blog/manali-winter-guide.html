Manali in Winter 2026 — Snow, Skiing & Complete Guide

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

Manali in winter is pure magic. Snow-covered streets, cozy cafes with bonfires, Solang Valley transformed into a ski resort, and the Himalayas wearing their thickest white coat. From December to February, Manali becomes India's favorite winter destination. This guide covers everything: snow months, skiing costs, what to wear, hotel prices, and how to plan the perfect winter trip.

👕 What to Wear in Manali Winter

  • Thermal innerwear (top + bottom) — non-negotiable. ₹500-1,000 at Decathlon.
  • Heavy down jacket or parka — temperatures drop to -5°C at night.
  • Waterproof snow boots — regular shoes will get wet and freeze. Rent at Solang (₹200-300) or buy in Manali.
  • Woolen cap, gloves, muffler, earmuffs — cover everything. Frostbite on ears and fingers is real.
  • Multiple layers: Thermal → T-shirt → Sweater → Fleece → Jacket. You can remove layers indoors.
  • Sunglasses — snow blindness is painful. The reflection off fresh snow is intense.
  • Moisturizer & lip balm — cold wind dries skin very fast.

🎄 Christmas & New Year in Manali

Christmas and New Year week (Dec 24 – Jan 2) is peak winter season. Here's what changes:

  • Hotel prices: 2-3x normal rates. A ₹2,000 room becomes ₹5,000-6,000.
  • Bus tickets: Sell out 2-3 weeks in advance. Book on RedBus early.
  • Old Manali: Special Christmas dinners, bonfires, live music at cafes.
  • Mall Road: Decorated, festive atmosphere, extended shopping hours.

Budget tip: Visit in the first week of January or late February. Same snow, 40-50% cheaper hotels, fewer crowds.

🏨 Best Areas to Stay in Winter

  • Old Manali: Best for snow experience, cafes, budget stays. Roads can get blocked in heavy snow — be prepared to walk.
  • Mall Road / Aleo: Better road access, closer to bus stand. Good for families and elderly.
  • Log Huts / Prini: Premium resorts with room heating, mountain views. ₹5,000-12,000/night.

⚠️ Winter Travel Tips

  1. Roads can close in heavy snow: The Mandi-Manali highway occasionally closes for a few hours during heavy snowfall. BRO clears it quickly, but carry food and water in the bus.
  2. Hot water is precious: Budget hotels may charge ₹100-200 extra for hot water buckets. Confirm before booking.
  3. Power cuts happen: Carry a power bank. Some hotels have generators, budget ones may not.
  4. Rohtang is CLOSED: You cannot visit Rohtang Pass in winter (Nov-May). Solang Valley is your snow destination.
  5. Start your bus journey early: Daytime buses are safer in winter. Night buses can face delays due to fog and ice.
