Manali to Kasol Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: August 2026 · ⏱️ 9 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🌿 From Mountains to Magic Valley: The journey from Manali to Kasol is a short but stunning 80 km ride that takes you from the bustling hill station into the heart of Parvati Valley — Himachal's backpacker and hippie paradise. Kasol is famous for its Israeli cafes, riverside camps, trekking trails, and the nearby hot springs of Manikaran. This guide covers every way to travel, costs, a perfect 2-day itinerary, and extensions to Tosh and Kheerganga.

🗺️ The Route: Manali → Kasol (80 km)

Distance: ~80 km | Travel Time: 2.5-4 hours (depending on mode) | Route: Manali → Kullu → Bhuntar → Kasol | Road Condition: Generally good, well-paved throughout

📍 0 km
Manali (2,050m) — Starting Point
Start from Manali. Buses to Kullu/Bhuntar leave from the Manali HRTC Bus Stand near Mall Road throughout the day. If taking a taxi, they are available at the taxi stand near the bus stand. The road descends alongside the Beas River through beautiful pine forests.
📍 40 km
Kullu (1,279m) — Valley of Gods
The district headquarters. Famous for Kullu shawls and the Dussehra festival. If you have time, visit the Raghunath Temple and the Kullu shawl market. Good place for a tea break. The road from Kullu to Bhuntar is flat and runs along the river.
📍 50 km
Bhuntar — Road Junction
Key junction. The road splits at Bhuntar. The left fork continues to Mandi and Chandigarh. The RIGHT fork enters Parvati Valley towards Kasol and Manikaran. Bhuntar also has the nearest airport (Kullu-Manali Airport, also known as Bhuntar Airport). If you're on a bus to Kasol, it will turn right here into the Parvati Valley.
📍 65 km
Jari — Gateway to Parvati Valley
A small market town. The Parvati River becomes clearly visible from here — turquoise blue and rushing. Basic dhabas and tea stalls. The valley narrows after Jari, with steep mountain walls on both sides.
📍 80 km
🏁 Kasol (1,580m) — Welcome to Mini Israel!
You've arrived in Kasol — the heart of Parvati Valley! Known for its Israeli cafes, laid-back vibe, riverside camping, and stunning mountain setting. Check into your hotel, guesthouse, or riverside camp. Walk along the Parvati River in the evening. Try the famous Israeli food at one of the many cafes. Kasol is small — you can walk everywhere.

Direct Bus (Manali → Kasol)3-3.5 hrs₹200-300Some HRTC and private buses go directly to Kasol/Manikaran. Check at Manali bus stand for timings. Less frequent than Bhuntar buses.
Shared Taxi (Manali → Bhuntar)2 hrs₹250-350Shared Sumo/Bolero from Manali taxi stand. From Bhuntar, another shared taxi to Kasol (₹50-80). Total: ₹300-430. Faster than bus.
Private Taxi (Manali → Kasol)2-2.5 hrs₹2,000-3,000 (total)Private cab, door-to-door. Comfortable for families or groups. Can be split among 3-4 people.

Recommendation: The HRTC bus is the cheapest and a perfectly comfortable option. Take a Manali→Bhuntar bus (₹120-180), then a local bus or shared taxi from Bhuntar to Kasol (₹30-80). Total journey: 3-4 hours, under ₹300 per person.

🗓️ Suggested 2-Day Manali to Kasol Itinerary

Day 1
Manali → Kasol — Arrival & Exploration
Morning: Take an early bus from Manali (8-9 AM) to Bhuntar, then connect to Kasol. Reach by late morning/noon. Check into your guesthouse or camp. Afternoon: Walk along the Parvati River. Explore Kasol market — small but charming with Israeli cafes, bakeries, and souvenir shops. Evening: Dinner at an Israeli cafe. Try shakshuka, hummus, or wood-fired pizza. The riverside cafes with fairy lights create a magical atmosphere at night.
Day 2
Manikaran + Return to Manali
Morning: Take a local bus or shared taxi to Manikaran (5 km, ₹30-50, 15 min). Visit the famous Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara — a beautiful Sikh temple with natural hot water springs. Take a dip in the hot spring (separate pools for men and women). The langar (community meal) at the gurudwara is free and delicious. Visit the Shiva temple next to the gurudwara. Afternoon: Return to Kasol. Have lunch. Late afternoon: Take the bus back to Manali. Reach by evening.

♨️ Manikaran Sahib — Hot Springs & Gurudwara

Manikaran is located just 5 km from Kasol and is the highlight of any trip to Parvati Valley. The main attraction is the Manikaran Sahib Gurudwara, a beautiful Sikh temple built over natural hot water springs. According to legend, the hot springs were created when the serpent king Sheshnag released the boiling water after Mata Parvati lost her earring (mani) here — hence the name Manikaran.

  • Hot springs: Separate bathing pools for men and women. The water is naturally hot (around 40-45°C) and rich in sulphur — believed to have healing properties. Carry a towel.
  • Langar: The gurudwara serves free community meals (langar) to all visitors. Simple but delicious — dal, rice, roti, and sabzi. A humbling and beautiful experience.
  • Shiva Temple: Right next to the gurudwara is an ancient Shiva temple worth visiting.
  • How to reach: Local buses (₹30-50) and shared taxis (₹50-80) run frequently between Kasol and Manikaran. It's a 15-minute drive.

🏔️ Extend Your Trip: Tosh & Kheerganga

If you have more than 2 days, Parvati Valley offers incredible extensions:

Tosh Village (21 km from Kasol)

A quiet, picturesque village at the end of the Parvati Valley road. Known for its laid-back vibe, stunning mountain views, and cafes that are even more relaxed than Kasol. Shared taxis from Kasol to Tosh take about 45 minutes (₹100-150). Stay overnight at a guesthouse or homestay (₹500-1,200). Tosh is the perfect place to do absolutely nothing — just sit, read, and stare at the mountains.

Kheerganga Trek (from Barshaini)

One of the most popular treks in Himachal. A 12 km climb from Barshaini (near Tosh) to a high-altitude meadow at 2,960m with natural hot water pools. The trek takes 4-6 hours one way. Best done as an overnight trip — stay at the basic camps at the top (₹300-500). The hot pool at Kheerganga with a mountain backdrop is one of those "bucket list" experiences.

⚠️ Travel Tips for Kasol

  1. Carry cash: ATMs in Kasol can be unreliable. Carry enough cash from Manali or Kullu.
  2. Mobile network: Airtel and Jio work in Kasol. BSNL is most reliable in the valley.
  3. Respect local culture: Kasol and Parvati Valley are in Himachal. Dress modestly, especially when visiting Manikaran Gurudwara.
  4. Try Israeli food: Kasol is famous for it. Shakshuka, falafel, hummus, and freshly baked bread are must-tries.
  5. Book stays in advance during peak season: May-June and October are busy. Riverside camps fill up quickly.
  6. Kheerganga trek preparation: Start early (by 7 AM). Carry water, snacks, rain gear, and a torch. The trail is well-marked.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kasol from Manali?

Kasol is approximately 80 km from Manali. The journey takes 2.5-4 hours depending on your mode of transport and road conditions.

How can I go from Manali to Kasol?

The cheapest way is the HRTC bus from Manali to Bhuntar (₹120-180) and then a local bus or shared taxi from Bhuntar to Kasol (₹30-80). Shared taxis and private cabs are also available for a faster but more expensive journey.

Is Kasol worth visiting from Manali?

Absolutely. Kasol offers a completely different vibe from Manali — laid-back, bohemian, and culturally unique with its Israeli influence. The nearby Manikaran hot springs and the option to extend to Tosh or Kheerganga make it a highly worthwhile 2-3 day extension to any Manali trip.

What is the cheapest way from Manali to Kasol?

The HRTC bus via Bhuntar is the cheapest option. Total cost: approximately ₹150-230 per person one way. The journey takes about 3-3.5 hours including the change at Bhuntar.

How many days are enough for Kasol?

2 days minimum for Kasol and Manikaran. 3-4 days if you want to include Tosh village. 4-5 days if you want to do the Kheerganga trek as well.

Is there a direct bus from Manali to Kasol?

Some HRTC and private buses run directly from Manali to Kasol/Manikaran, but they are less frequent. Most travelers take a bus to Bhuntar and change there — which is easy and well-connected.
