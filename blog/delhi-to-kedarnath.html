Delhi to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 7 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🗽 Delhi Advantage: Delhi is the closest major city to Kedarnath. You can reach Haridwar in just 4-6 hours by train. Total trip can be done in 3-4 days — faster than from any other city. Multiple trains, buses, and even direct flights to Dehradun make this the easiest starting point for your Kedarnath Yatra.

🚂 Option 1: Delhi to Haridwar by Train (Cheapest)

Delhi has the best train connectivity to Haridwar of any city. Over 10 daily trains cover the 200 km distance in 4-6 hours. No overnight journey needed — you can leave in the morning and reach by afternoon.

Our recommendation: Take the Shatabdi Express (6:45 AM, ₹650) — you'll reach Haridwar by 10:15 AM, fresh and ready to catch a bus to the mountains the same morning. For budget travelers, Jan Shatabdi 2S at ₹150-200 is unbeatable value.

Where to Board in Delhi

  • New Delhi Railway Station (NDLS): Most Shatabdi trains leave from here. Metro Yellow Line. Platform 1 for most Haridwar trains.
  • Delhi Junction / Old Delhi (DLI): Some overnight trains. Metro: Chandni Chowk.
  • Hazrat Nizamuddin (NZM): A few Haridwar-bound trains. South Delhi.

🚌 Option 2: Delhi to Haridwar by Bus

  • Government Buses (UTC): From ISBT Kashmere Gate. ₹350-500/person. 5-6 hours.
  • Private Volvo/AC Buses: From ISBT Kashmere Gate or Anand Vihar. ₹600-1,000/person. Book on RedBus.
  • Shared Taxi: From ISBT Kashmere Gate. ₹500-700/person. 4-5 hours.

🏨 Where to Stay

  • Haridwar: Hotel Ganga Lahari (₹1,500-2,500), GMVN (₹800-1,500).
  • Rishikesh: Budget hotels near bus stand ₹500-1,500/night.
  • Kedarnath: GMVN (₹800-2,500), lodges (₹600-1,000), Kedar Valley Resort (₹5,000-10,000).

⚠️ Common Mistakes Delhi Travelers Make

  1. Not booking Shatabdi early: Sells out 1-2 weeks ahead in peak season.
  2. Wrong station: NDLS, DLI, NZM are different — check your ticket.
  3. Starting too late from Haridwar: Mountain buses leave 5-8 AM only.
  4. Not carrying warm clothes: Delhi 40°C ≠ Kedarnath 5°C at night.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kedarnath from Delhi?

Approximately 450 km. Delhi is the closest major city to Kedarnath.

Can I do Kedarnath from Delhi in 3 days?

Yes! Day 1: Delhi→mountains. Day 2: Trek. Day 3: Darshan + return.

Cheapest way from Delhi to Kedarnath?

Jan Shatabdi 2S (₹150) + bus + trek. Under ₹6,000 round trip.
