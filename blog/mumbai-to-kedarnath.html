Mumbai to Kedarnath Complete Travel Guide 2026

📅 Updated: July 2026 · ⏱️ 6 min read · ✍️ By Kumar Raj

🌊 Mumbai Special: Mumbai is ~1,800 km from Kedarnath — one of the longest journeys on Yatra. The train ride alone is 24-30 hours. But there's good news: direct flights from Mumbai to Dehradun can get you to the mountains in just 2 hours. This guide covers both the budget train route and the time-saving flight option.

🚂 Option 1: Mumbai to Haridwar by Train (Budget)

Trains to Haridwar depart from multiple Mumbai stations. The journey is long but direct — no need to change trains.

Our recommendation: Take the Haridwar Express (12:45 PM, 3AC ₹2,000-2,500). It's the fastest option from Mumbai and arrives at Haridwar around 1 PM the next day. Book 1-2 months ahead — Mumbai trains are always in high demand.

Which Station in Mumbai?

  • Bandra Terminus (BDTS): Haridwar Express and Dehradun Express leave from here. Western line — accessible from Bandra, Andheri, Borivali.
  • Lokmanya Tilak Terminus (LTT): Some Haridwar trains. Central line — Kurla area.
  • Mumbai Central (BCT): A few options. Check your ticket carefully.

⚠️ Mumbai-Specific Tips

  1. Book trains 2 months ahead: Mumbai-Haridwar routes are some of the busiest in India. 3AC and 2AC sell out weeks in advance during May-June.
  2. Bandra Terminus is well-connected: Take a local train to Bandra, then auto to the station. Arrive 1 hour early — Bandra Terminus can be crowded.
  3. Flight is worth considering: A 24-hour train vs a 2-hour flight. If you value time, the Premium Plan is a game-changer from Mumbai.
  4. Carry food for the long journey: The 24-hour train ride is easier with your own snacks. The pantry car is available but limited.
  5. Weather shock: Mumbai's humidity vs Kedarnath's cold. Carry a warm jacket even if you're sweating at Bandra station.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

How far is Kedarnath from Mumbai?

Approximately 1,800 km. One of the longest routes on Yatra.

Direct train from Mumbai to Kedarnath?

No. Nearest railhead is Haridwar. Direct trains from Bandra/LTT to Haridwar — no change needed.

Train or flight from Mumbai?

Train: 24-30 hours, ₹800-5,000. Flight: 2 hours, ₹10,000-16,000. Choose based on budget and time.

How many days from Mumbai?

Minimum 5-6 days by train, or 3-4 days with flights (Premium Plan).
