Yes, ₹10,000 is enough if you're traveling from nearby cities like Delhi, Lucknow, or Jaipur using general class train tickets, budget accommodation (lodges/tents), and simple dhaba meals.

From Delhi, the total can be as low as ₹6,500–8,000 per person for a 4-day trip. From Lucknow or Jaipur, expect ₹7,000–9,000.

From farther cities: If you're traveling from Bhagalpur, Patna, Kolkata, or Mumbai, the train journey is longer and costs more. Budget ₹7,000–9,000 from Bihar cities, and ₹9,000–12,000 from Kolkata/Mumbai.

What ₹10,000 covers: Round-trip train (general/sleeper), bus/jeep transfers, trek (free), 2 meals/day at dhabas, 1 night stay at Kedarnath, and miscellaneous expenses.

What it doesn't cover: Helicopter, pony, palki, premium hotels, or flight tickets. Those are extras — see our Plan Page to customize.
The trek is 16 km from Gaurikund to Kedarnath with a climb from 6,500 ft to 11,755 ft. It's moderately difficult — not technical, but physically demanding.

For elderly or less fit travelers: Ponies (₹5,000 round trip), Palkis/Dolis (₹16,000 round trip), and Helicopters (₹15,000 round trip) are available. Thousands of senior citizens complete the yatra every year using these services.

If trekking, walk slowly, take breaks every 2-3 km, and stay hydrated. See our Route Map for detailed checkpoint distances.
Temple opens: April/May (Akshaya Tritiya) · Closes: October/November (Bhai Dooj)

• Best weather: May–June & September–October
• Cheapest time: Mid-September to early October (less crowd, lower hotel prices)
• Avoid: July–August (heavy monsoon, landslide risk)
• Peak crowd: May–June (school holidays)

Yes, registration is mandatory. Without it, you cannot enter beyond Gaurikund.

Online: Visit registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in — create an account with your mobile number, fill in traveler details, upload ID proof, and download your e-pass.

Offline: Registration counters at Haridwar, Rishikesh, Guptkashi, and Sonprayag.

Documents to carry: Aadhar card, e-pass (printed or mobile), passport-size photo, medical certificate (for elderly).
Essentials:
• Warm clothes (jacket, thermal, gloves, woolen cap) — it gets very cold at night even in summer
• Comfortable trekking shoes (break them in before the trip)
• Raincoat/poncho (weather changes suddenly)
• Water bottle (2L), ORS packets, dry fruits, chocolates
• Power bank (no charging points on the trek)
• Torch/headlamp for early morning
• Basic medicines (pain reliever, band-aids, Diamox for altitude)
• Trekking stick (buy at Gaurikund for ₹50-80)

Only BSNL works reliably after Gaurikund. Airtel and Jio stop working within the first 2-3 km of the trek. BSNL has coverage at Rambara (10 km) and patchy coverage near Kedarnath.

Tip: Inform your family before starting the trek that you'll be unreachable for 1-2 days. Carry a BSNL SIM if staying connected is important.
Yes. Helicopters operate from Guptkashi, Phata, and Sirsi to Kedarnath.

• Cost: ₹7,000–8,000 per person one way
• Booking: Only through IRCTC or the official Uttarakhand tourism portal. Do NOT book through unknown agents or social media ads — many are scams.
• Timing: Flights operate 6 AM–4 PM, weather permitting. Book 1-2 months in advance during peak season.
• Weight limit: 5 kg baggage per person.
Minimum: 4 days from most cities (2 days travel + 1 day trek up + 1 day darshan & return).
Premium (flight): 2 days possible from Delhi/Patna.
Comfortable pace: 5-6 days if you want to explore nearby places like Bhairavnath Temple, Gandhi Sarovar, or Triyuginarayan.
