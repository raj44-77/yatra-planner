From Delhi: Basic Plan ₹5,500-7,000/person.

Yes, ₹10,000 is enough from nearby cities like Delhi, Lucknow, or Jaipur using general class train, budget stay, and simple meals. From farther cities like Kolkata or Mumbai, budget ₹12,000-15,000.
The trek is 16 km from Gaurikund to Kedarnath climbing from 6,500 ft to 11,755 ft. Moderately difficult. Ponies (₹5,000 round trip), Palkis (₹16,000), and Helicopters (₹15,000) available for those who cannot trek.
May–June & September–October. Cheapest: mid-September to early October. Avoid July–August (monsoon, landslides).

🛕 Badrinath Yatra

From Delhi: Basic ₹5,000-7,000/person. From Bhagalpur/Patna: ₹6,000-8,000/person. Badrinath is cheaper than Kedarnath — no trekking needed. Use our Badrinath Plan Page.
No trekking at all! The road goes all the way to the temple gate. Ideal for elderly travelers and families with young children.
Route: Your City → Haridwar → Rishikesh → Joshimath → Badrinath. From Joshimath, shared jeeps run every 20-30 min (45 km, 1 hour). See Badrinath Plan.
Kedarnath: Lord Shiva · 16 km trek · 3,583m · Physically demanding.
Badrinath: Lord Vishnu · Road access · 3,300m · Much easier. Tapt Kund hot spring. Mana Village nearby. Both are Char Dham.

🏔️ Manali Trip

From Delhi: Basic ₹2,500-3,500/person (3-4 days). From Patna/Mumbai/Kolkata: ₹4,000-6,000/person. Use our Manali Plan Page for exact costs.
No mandatory trekking. Manali is a road destination — buses and cabs go everywhere. There are optional treks (Hampta Pass, Bhrigu Lake) for adventure lovers, but you can enjoy Manali without any trekking.
Year-round destination! Summer (Mar-Jun): pleasant weather, adventure sports. Monsoon (Jul-Aug): lush green, fewer crowds. Winter (Dec-Feb): snow, skiing, honeymoon season.
