Day 2
Explore Manali — Mall Road, Hadimba Temple & Old Manali
🏨 Check into Hostel/Guesthouse
Budget stays in Old Manali or near Mall Road. ₹400-800/night. Recommended: Zostel Manali, Backpacker's Inn, or local guesthouses in Old Manali.
₹400 – 800 / night
🚶 Walk to Hadimba Devi Temple
Ancient wooden temple surrounded by cedar forest. Built in 1553. 2 km from Mall Road — pleasant 20-30 min walk. Free entry. Beautiful architecture and peaceful surroundings.
₹0 (Free Entry)
🛍️ Mall Road & Old Manali
Walk along Mall Road for shopping (woolen clothes, handicrafts, Tibetan items). Cross the bridge to Old Manali — cafes, bakeries, laid-back vibe. Great for budget travelers.
₹0 (Self-explored)
🍱 Food — Day 2
Breakfast at a local dhaba (aloo paratha + chai ₹60-80). Lunch at Old Manali cafe (₹100-150). Dinner at Mall Road dhaba (₹150-200).
₹300 – 400 / person
🌙 Night at Manali hostel/guesthouse. ₹400-800. Old Manali has the best budget stays with mountain views. Hot water available (may cost ₹50-100 extra).
Day 3
Solang Valley — Adventure & Snow
🚌 Manali → Solang Valley (Shared Taxi/Bus)
14 km from Manali. Shared taxi from Mall Road: ₹100-150/person. HRTC bus also available: ₹50-80. 45 min drive. Stunning mountain views on the way.
₹100 – 150 / person
🏔️ Explore Solang Valley
Famous for snow (Dec-Mar), paragliding (Apr-Nov), zorbing, horse riding, and cable car. Even if you don't do activities, the valley views are worth the trip. Snow-capped peaks visible year-round.
₹0 (Entry free, activities extra)
🍱 Food — Day 3
Breakfast at Manali dhaba (₹60-80). Lunch at Solang Valley dhaba (₹100-150). Dinner back at Manali (₹150-200).
₹300 – 400 / person
🌙 Night at Manali. If you have extra days, visit Rohtang Pass (51 km, ₹500-800 shared taxi), Naggar Castle, or Jogini Waterfall.
Day 4

Board return bus from Manali bus stand. Evening departure, reach next morning. ₹700-1,000/person.
₹700 – 1,000 / person
🚂 Delhi/Chandigarh → Your City
Return train to your city. Refer to Day 1 reverse.
₹350 – 500 / person
🍱 Food — Day 4
Breakfast in Manali (₹60-80). Lunch at bus stop (₹100-150). Dinner on the way (₹100-150).
₹250 – 350 / person
