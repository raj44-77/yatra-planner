📍 Route: Delhi → Manali

~550 km · 12-14 hrs · Road all the way — no trekking!

1
🚌 Delhi (ISBT Kashmere Gate)
Starting point · Altitude: 216m
HRTC and private buses to Manali. Metro: Kashmere Gate (Red/Yellow/Violet lines). Platform 35-38 for HRTC buses.
0 km · Starting Point
2
🍽️ Murthal
Famous highway dhaba strip · 50 km from Delhi
Paratha stop! Multiple 24/7 dhabas. Amrik Sukhdev, Gulshan, Mannat. Good for dinner/snacks.
50 km · 1 hr
3
🏙️ Chandigarh
The planned city · Altitude: 321m
Buses stop at Sector 43 ISBT. Clean restrooms. Food court available. 250 km from Delhi.
250 km · 5 hrs
4
🏘️ Mandi
Gateway to Kullu Valley · Altitude: 760m
Beautiful town on Beas River. Chai and snack stop. Ancient Shiva temple. Last major town before mountains.
400 km · 8-9 hrs
5
🏘️ Kullu
Valley of Gods · Altitude: 1,279m
Famous for Kullu shawls and Dussehra festival. Beautiful valley views. 40 km before Manali.
510 km · 11 hrs
🏔️

Hill Station Paradise! · Altitude: 2,050m
Mall Road, Old Manali, Hadimba Temple, Solang Valley, Rohtang Pass. Adventure sports, snow, cafes, and Himalayan views. 🏔️

Kedarnath Temple

Helipad

📍 Important Locations in Manali

🏔️
Solang Valley
Adventure hub. Paragliding, skiing, zorbing. 14 km from Manali.
🏛️
Hadimba Temple
Ancient wooden temple. Cedar forest. 2 km walk.
🛍️
Mall Road
Shopping, cafes, restaurants. Heart of Manali.
🏘️
Old Manali
Hippie cafes, backpacker hostels, laid-back vibe.
🏥
Civil Hospital
Near Mall Road. Emergency medical help.
🚌
HRTC Bus Stand
Main bus stand. Buses to Delhi, Chandigarh, Leh.

🚨 Emergency Route Information

🚁 Helicopter Evacuation

Helipad near temple. Contact helpline immediately in emergency.

🏥 Nearest Hospital

PHC Sonprayag (Kedarnath) / Joshimath (Badrinath) / Civil Hospital Manali. AIIMS Rishikesh for serious cases.
