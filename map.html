📍 Complete Route: Haridwar → Kedarnath

~270 km · 2 days (one way) · Click any stop to mark your location

1
🚂 Haridwar (HW)
Railway Station · Altitude: 314m
Starting point. Buses and shared taxis to Rishikesh available outside Platform 1.
0 km · Starting Point
2
🚌 Rishikesh
Yatra Bus Stand (Tehri Bus Stand) · Altitude: 372m
Gateway to the mountains. UTC and private buses to Sonprayag depart 5 AM-9 AM. Shared taxis also available.
20 km · 45 min
3
🛕 Devprayag
Bhagirathi + Alaknanda Sangam · Altitude: 472m
Holy confluence forming the Ganga. Temple at the sangam. Buses stop 15-20 min for photos and tea.
70 km · ~2 hrs
4
🏘️ Srinagar
Largest town on route · Altitude: 579m
Lunch stop (20-30 min). Good dhabas: "Srinagar Dhaba", "Garhwal Bhojanalaya". Last reliable ATMs before Kedarnath — withdraw cash here.
140 km · ~4.5 hrs
5
🛕 Rudraprayag
Alaknanda + Mandakini Sangam · Altitude: 610m
Second holy confluence. Roads become narrower and more winding after this point. Beautiful valley views.
180 km · ~6 hrs
6
🏘️ Guptkashi
Last major town · Altitude: 1,319m
Hotels, dhabas, medical stores, petrol pump. Helicopter booking counter for Kedarnath. Many pilgrims stay overnight here to break the journey.
210 km · ~7.5 hrs
7
🚙 Sonprayag
Vehicle Terminus · Altitude: 1,829m
All private vehicles must stop here. Shared jeeps to Gaurikund (₹60-80). Lodges available if arriving late (₹500-800). Parking available if you brought your own vehicle.
220 km · 8-10 hrs
8
🚙 Gaurikund
🚨 Registration Check Post · Altitude: 1,982m
Show e-pass here (printed or mobile). Shops for trekking sticks (₹50-100), raincoats, water, snacks. Pony (₹2,000-3,000) and Palki (₹4,000-5,000) booking counters. Gauri Kund hot spring — many take a holy dip here.
225 km · 20 min from Sonprayag
T1
🥾 Jungle Chatti
First rest stop on trek · Altitude: 2,300m
4-5 small dhabas — hot tea (₹20), Maggi (₹50-80), biscuits, water. Basic toilets (₹5-10). Shaded area with stone benches. Rest 10-15 min.
4 km trek · ~1.5 hrs
T2
🥾 Bheembali
Larger resting point · Altitude: 2,600m
Proper dhabas with seating. Aloo paratha (₹60-80), tea, light meals. Toilets available. Climb gets noticeably steeper after this. Rest 20-30 min.
7 km trek · ~3 hrs
T3
🥾 Rambara
Halfway Point — Best Lunch Stop · Altitude: 2,900m
Multiple dhabas: "Shiva Dhaba" and "Himalayan Cafe" most popular. Thali ₹150-200. Small temple. Last reliable water refill point. Rest 30-40 min. Mobile network (BSNL only) available here.
10 km trek · ~4.5 hrs
T4
🥾 Lincholi
Final stop before temple · Altitude: 3,200m
Few dhabas, water point. Steepest section of the trek starts here — the last 3 km are the hardest. Kedarnath temple becomes visible from here — huge morale boost! Rest 10 min, drink water, push for the final climb.
13 km trek · ~6 hrs
🕉️
🛕 Kedarnath Temple — YOU MADE IT!
Sacred Jyotirlinga · Altitude: 3,583m
One of the 12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva. Surrounded by snow-capped peaks — Kedarnath Peak (6,940m), Kedar Dome (6,831m). Temple, lodges, dhabas all within 300m radius. Bhairavnath Temple 500m behind. You completed the pilgrimage! 🕉️
16 km trek · 7-8 hrs total

📍 Important Locations at Kedarnath

🛕
Kedarnath Temple
Main shrine. Morning aarti 4:30 AM. Darshan 6 AM-3 PM & 5 PM-9 PM.
🗿
Bhairavnath Temple
500m behind main temple. Guardian deity. Best mountain photos from here at sunrise.
🏨
GMVN Guest House
Government-run stay. Clean rooms ₹800-2,500/night. 200m from temple. Hot water available.
🏥
Medical Aid Post
Near temple entrance. Basic medical help, oxygen cylinders, first aid. Open 24 hrs during season.
🚁
Helipad
1 km from temple. Helicopter services to Guptkashi/Phata. Book via IRCTC or heliyatra.com.
💧
Water Point
Free drinking water near temple entrance. Carry bottles to refill before trekking down.

🚨 Emergency Route Information

🚁 Helicopter Evacuation

Helipad: 1 km from temple. Book via IRCTC or at Guptkashi counter. In emergency, contact Kedarnath Helpline immediately.

🏥 Nearest Hospital

Primary Health Centre at Sonprayag (20 km from Gaurikund). For serious emergencies: Rudraprayag District Hospital (180 km) or AIIMS Rishikesh (250 km).

📞 Emergency Numbers

Disaster Management: 0135-2710334

🗺️ Alternate Route (Landslide Backup)

If main path blocked: Gaurikund → Rambara bypass → Kedarnath (longer but safer during landslides). Always ask locals at Gaurikund for current conditions before starting.
