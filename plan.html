Day 2
Haridwar → Rishikesh → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath (The Mountain Climb)
🚌 Haridwar to Rishikesh — Public Bus (45-60 min, 20 km)
From Haridwar Railway Station: Exit from the main gate (Platform 1 side). Walk straight for exactly 200 meters — you'll see the Haridwar Roadways Bus Depot right opposite the station. You cannot miss it.

Which bus to take: Look for buses with "Rishikesh" written on the front windshield. They leave every 15-20 minutes starting from 5:00 AM. The bus conductor will shout "Rishikesh! Rishikesh!" — just ask him to confirm.

Ticket: Buy from the conductor after boarding. ₹70-100 per person. No advance booking needed. Keep small change ready (₹100 notes work best).

Where to get down: Tell the conductor "Rishikesh Yatra Bus Stand" or "Tehri Bus Stand". This is the main bus stand where buses to Sonprayag/Kedarnath depart. Don't get down at earlier Rishikesh stops — go all the way to the last stop.

Alternative — Shared Auto: Shared auto-rickshaws wait outside Haridwar station. ₹100-150 per person, 35-40 minutes. They drop you at the same bus stand.
₹70 – 100 / person
🚌 Rishikesh to Sonprayag — Bus (8-10 hours, ~220 km)
From Rishikesh Yatra Bus Stand: Look for buses with "Sonprayag" or "Gaurikund" signboards. Two types: UTC Government Buses (green, cheaper at ₹800-900, more stops) and Private Buses (₹1,000-1,200, faster, fewer stops).

Buying tickets: At the ticket counter (open from 5 AM) or directly from the conductor. Catch a bus before 7 AM to reach Sonprayag by 3-4 PM.

The Route: Rishikesh → Devprayag (70 km, Bhagirathi+Alaknanda sangam, photo stop) → Srinagar (140 km, lunch stop, good dhabas) → Rudraprayag (180 km, Alaknanda+Mandakini sangam) → Guptkashi (210 km, last town) → Sonprayag (220 km, final stop).

Important: If you reach Sonprayag after 6 PM, shared jeeps to Gaurikund reduce. Stay overnight at Sonprayag (lodges: ₹500-800) and continue next morning.
₹800 – 1,200 / person
🚙 Sonprayag to Gaurikund — Shared Jeep (20-25 min, 5 km)
At Sonprayag: Walk to the shared jeep stand near the bus parking. Jeeps leave when full (8-10 passengers). ₹60-80/person. Pay the driver or at the small ticket counter.

Registration Check at Gaurikund: Keep your e-pass ready (phone or printed). The police check post scans your QR code. No registration = no entry.

At Gaurikund: Shops for trekking sticks (₹50-100), raincoats (₹50-200), water bottles (₹20-30). Pony/palki booking counters available. The Gauri Kund hot spring is here — many pilgrims take a dip before trekking.
₹60 – 80 / person
🥾 Gaurikund to Kedarnath — The Sacred Trek (16 km, 5-8 hours)
This is the journey you came for! Well-paved stone path, wide enough for 4-5 people. You cannot get lost — just follow the crowd.

📍 Gaurikund (0 km, 6,500 ft): Start point. Fill water bottles, buy trekking stick (₹50-80, highly recommended), use toilet (₹10). Start by 6-7 AM if you stayed overnight, or by 2-3 PM if same day.

📍 Jungle Chatti (4 km, ~1.5 hrs): First rest stop. 4-5 dhabas — hot tea (₹20), Maggi (₹50-80), biscuits. Rest 10-15 min. Toilets available (₹5-10).

📍 Bheembali (7 km, ~3 hrs): Larger dhabas. The climb gets steeper after this. Have aloo paratha (₹60-80), tea. Toilets available. Rest 20-30 min.

📍 Rambara (10 km, ~4.5 hrs): Halfway point! Best place for lunch. "Shiva Dhaba" and "Himalayan Cafe" — thali ₹150-200. Rest 30-40 min. Refill water (₹30 for Bisleri).

📍 Lincholi (13 km, ~6 hrs): Last stop before Kedarnath. Steepest section ahead. Rest 10 min, drink water. Kedarnath temple visible from here — a huge morale boost!

📍 Kedarnath Base (16 km, ~7-8 hrs): You made it! Temple surrounded by snow-capped peaks. Find your lodge, freshen up, attend evening aarti (6-7 PM).

Critical Tips: Walk slowly, take breaks every 2-3 km. Stay hydrated — dehydration worsens altitude sickness. Only BSNL mobile works after Rambara. Ponies (₹2,000-3,000) and palkis (₹4,000-5,000) available at Gaurikund if needed. Weather can change suddenly — carry a poncho.
₹0 (Trek is free)
🍱 Food — Day 2
Breakfast (Haridwar/Rishikesh): Dhabas near bus stands. Aloo-puri, chai, paratha — ₹50-80.
Lunch (Srinagar): Bus stops 20-30 min. Garhwali thali at local dhaba — ₹100-150.
Dinner (Kedarnath): After reaching, dhabas near temple. Thali ₹150-200. Try khichdi — light and warm after the trek.
Must carry: Dry fruits, chocolates, ORS packets (Electral ₹20), water bottles.
₹360 – 400 / person
🌙 Night at Kedarnath. Lodge/Tent: ₹600-1,000/person. GMVN Guest House recommended (₹800, clean, government-run). Most lodges within 200m of temple. Basic rooms with bed, blanket, shared bathroom. Hot water: ₹50-100 per bucket. Very cold at night (5-10°C) — carry thermals/warm jacket. Power bank essential — electricity can be erratic.
Day 3
Kedarnath Darshan → Return Journey to Haridwar
🛕 Morning Darshan at Kedarnath Temple
Temple Timings: Opens 4:00 AM. Morning Aarti: 4:30 AM — do not miss this! Main darshan: 6:00 AM – 3:00 PM, then 5:00 PM – 9:00 PM (temple closes 3-5 PM for bhog).

Tips for the best experience: Wake up at 3:30 AM, freshen up (hot water available at most lodges in the morning), reach temple by 4:15 AM. The early morning aarti in the Himalayas is a divine experience — fewer crowds, the sound of chants echoing off the mountains.

After darshan: Walk 500 meters behind the main temple to Bhairavnath Temple — the guardian deity of Kedarnath. The view of the Kedarnath peak from here in the morning light is breathtaking. Best photos of your trip will be from this spot (5:30-7:00 AM golden light).

Rituals: Offer bilva patra, flowers, and water to the Jyotirlinga. Pandits available for special puja (₹500-2,000). Photography NOT allowed inside the temple.
₹0 (Darshan is free)
🥾 Kedarnath → Gaurikund — Trek Down (16 km, 4-6 hours)
Start by 7:00-8:00 AM after breakfast at your lodge or a nearby dhaba. The descent is faster and easier on the lungs, but harder on the knees — use your trekking stick!

Same route in reverse: Kedarnath → Lincholi (3 km) → Rambara (6 km, good stop for tea/snacks) → Bheembali (9 km) → Jungle Chatti (12 km, good stop for lunch) → Gaurikund (16 km).

Where to eat on the way down: Rambara has the best dhabas for a mid-morning break (tea, Maggi, paratha — ₹80-120). Jungle Chatti is perfect for lunch (thali ₹120-150) — it's the last proper food stop before Gaurikund.

Important: Go slow on steep sections — it's harder on knees going down. Trekking stick helps tremendously. Reach Gaurikund by 1:00-2:00 PM.
₹0 (Trek is free)
🚙 Gaurikund → Sonprayag (Shared Jeep, 20 min)
Shared jeeps from Gaurikund market. ₹60-80/person. 5 km, 20-25 minutes.
₹60 – 80 / person
🚌 Sonprayag → Rishikesh (Bus, 8-10 hours)
From Sonprayag bus stand: Buses to Rishikesh leave throughout the day. Last bus is around 4:00-5:00 PM — you must reach Sonprayag by 3:00 PM to catch one. Buy ticket at the counter or from conductor. ₹800-1,200/person.

If you miss the last bus: Take a shared taxi to Guptkashi (₹100-150, 30 min) — more buses available from there till 6-7 PM. Or stay overnight at Sonprayag/Guptkashi (₹500-800).

Journey: 8-10 hours back to Rishikesh. You'll reach by 11:00 PM – 1:00 AM.
₹800 – 1,200 / person
🚌 Rishikesh → Haridwar (Bus/Shared Auto, 45 min)
From Rishikesh bus stand, buses to Haridwar run even late at night. Or take shared auto (₹100-150). Reach Haridwar railway station.

Train from Haridwar: Kumbha Express (departs ~10:00 PM), Doon Express (departs ~11:00 PM). Buy general ticket at station counter (₹280-300 for Patna). Board train for overnight journey home.
₹70 – 100 / person
🍱 Food — Day 3
Breakfast at Kedarnath: Aloo-paratha, chai at lodge or dhaba — ₹80-100.
Lunch at Jungle Chatti: Simple thali or Maggi — ₹100-150.
Dinner at Haridwar: Before boarding train. Multiple restaurants near station. "Hoshiyar Puri" famous for kachori. ₹100-150.
₹300 / person
🌙 Night on train (Haridwar → Home). Board Kumbha Express (10 PM) or Doon Express (11 PM). General class ticket: ₹280-300. Journey: 17-18 hours. Reach your city tomorrow afternoon/evening.

Arriving at your city: The train reaches between 12:00 PM – 6:00 PM depending on which train you took and your city. Refer to Day 1 reverse for exact arrival times.

From station to home: Local auto, bus, or family pickup. Keep ₹100-200 for local transport.

Day 2
Haridwar → Rishikesh → Sonprayag → Gaurikund → Kedarnath (Shared Taxi + Comfort Trek)
🚕 Haridwar to Rishikesh — Shared Taxi (Sumo/Bolero, 35-40 min)
From Haridwar Railway Station: Exit Platform 1 side. You'll see a line of shared Sumo/Bolero/Tavera taxis parked outside — drivers shout "Rishikesh! Rishikesh!". Walk up to any one. ₹60-100/person. Leaves when 7-8 passengers are seated. Much faster and more comfortable than the bus.

Drop point: Rishikesh Yatra Bus Stand (Tehri Bus Stand). Journey: 35-40 minutes.

Private taxi option: If you want dedicated cab: ₹800-1,200 for Innova/Dzire. 25 minutes. Book via Ola/Uber or negotiate with drivers.

At Rishikesh: If you have 1-2 hours before the Sonprayag taxi, visit Triveni Ghat (5 min auto, ₹50) for morning Ganga Aarti. Or breakfast at Chotiwala Restaurant near Laxman Jhula (famous, ₹200-300/person).
₹60 – 100 / person
🚙 Rishikesh to Sonprayag — Shared Sumo/Bolero (7-8 hours, ~220 km)
From Rishikesh Yatra Bus Stand: Shared Sumo/Bolero taxis to Sonprayag line up near the bus stand entrance. They leave when 8-10 passengers are seated — typically every 30-45 minutes in the morning (5 AM – 9 AM).

Cost: ₹1,200-1,600/person. Pay the driver directly. Confirm price before sitting.

Why shared taxi is better than bus: Faster (7-8 hours vs 8-10), fewer stops, more legroom, drivers know the mountain roads well, they stop at scenic viewpoints for photos (Devprayag sangam, Rudraprayag confluence). Stops only for meals and toilet breaks — not at every village.

Meal stop: The driver stops at a good dhaba in Srinagar or Rudraprayag for 30 minutes. Cleaner and better food than bus-stop dhabas. Thali ₹200-250.

Important: If you reach Sonprayag after 5-6 PM, jeeps to Gaurikund reduce. Stay at Himalayan Paradise Hotel (₹2,000-3,000/night, good rooms with hot water) and continue fresh next morning.
₹1,200 – 1,600 / person
🚙 Sonprayag to Gaurikund — Shared Jeep (20-25 min, 5 km)
Shared jeep from Sonprayag parking. ₹60-80/person. Show registration e-pass at Gaurikund check post.
₹60 – 80 / person
🥾 Gaurikund to Kedarnath — Comfort Trek (16 km, 5-7 hours with planned breaks)
Same sacred trek — but with better planned breaks:

• Jungle Chatti (4 km): "Himalayan Cafe" — hot tea, Maggi, packaged snacks. ₹100-150. Rest 15 min.
• Bheembali (7 km): Sit-down dhaba with proper seating. Fresh paratha with curd, tea. ₹150-200. Rest 20 min.
• Rambara (10 km): "Shiva Dhaba" — best on the route. Clean, good thali ₹200-250. Rest 30 min.
• Lincholi (13 km): Final water/tea break. Steep climb ahead.

Comfort upgrades from Basic: Packaged water bottles (Bisleri ₹30 each at every stop). Energy bars, dry fruits, ORS. Good wooden trekking stick (₹80-120) instead of cheap bamboo. Reusable poncho (₹100-200) instead of plastic raincoat.

Estimated snack + water cost: ₹500/person for the entire trek.
₹500 / person (snacks + water)
🍱 Food — Day 2
Breakfast (Rishikesh): Chotiwala or Honey Hut restaurant. Proper breakfast — ₹200-300.
Lunch (Srinagar/Rudraprayag): Taxi stops at good dhaba. Thali ₹200-250.
Dinner (Kedarnath): "Kedar Restaurant" or "GMVN Canteen" — hygienic, good food. Thali ₹250-300. Try rajma-chawal — famous at this altitude!
₹500 – 600 / person
🌙 Night at Kedarnath — Private Room. ₹2,000-3,500/night (base for 1-2 people, +₹1,000 per extra person). Recommended: GMVN Kedarnath Guest House (₹2,500, attached bathroom, hot water), Bikaner House (₹2,000, basic but clean), Punjab Sindh Awas (₹3,000, better rooms). All provide blankets, hot water (morning/evening), charging points. Book through GMVN website or call — rooms fill fast in May-June.

After darshan, visit Bhairavnath Temple (500m behind) — the guardian deity. View of Kedarnath peak in morning light is spectacular.

Comfort tip: Most lodges allow you to keep luggage after checkout (leave by 10-11 AM). Freshen up, have breakfast, then start the trek down.
₹0 (Darshan is free)

केदारनाथ यात्रा की पूरी जानकारी

रजिस्ट्रेशन से लेकर ठहरने तक — वो सब कुछ जो आपको यात्रा से पहले जानना चाहिए।

📅

यात्रा कब शुरू होती है?

केदारनाथ धाम के कपाट हर साल अक्षय तृतीया (अप्रैल-मई) के आसपास खुलते हैं और भाई दूज (अक्टूबर-नवंबर) के आसपास बंद होते हैं।

🔥 सबसे ज्यादा भीड़: मई – जून

💰 सबसे सस्ता समय: सितंबर मध्य – अक्टूबर शुरुआत

⚠️ बरसात: जुलाई-अगस्त में भूस्खलन का खतरा

📝

रजिस्ट्रेशन कैसे करें?

केदारनाथ यात्रा के लिए रजिस्ट्रेशन अनिवार्य है।

🖥️ ऑनलाइन: registrationandtouristcare.uk.gov.in

🏢 ऑफलाइन: हरिद्वार, ऋषिकेश, गुप्तकाशी, सोनप्रयाग के केंद्रों पर।

🆔

कौन-से दस्तावेज साथ रखें?

  • आधार कार्ड या वैध फोटो आईडी
  • रजिस्ट्रेशन ई-पास (प्रिंट और मोबाइल में)
  • पासपोर्ट साइज फोटो
  • मेडिकल फिटनेस प्रमाणपत्र (बुजुर्गों के लिए)
⚠️

स्कैमर्स से कैसे बचें?

  • केवल आधिकारिक वेबसाइट से रजिस्ट्रेशन करें।
  • हेलीकॉप्टर टिकट केवल IRCTC से बुक करें।
  • "VIP दर्शन" के झांसे में न आएं।
  • किसी अनजान के खाते में भुगतान न करें।
🎒

पहली बार जाने वालों की तैयारी

  • गर्म कपड़े, जैकेट, टोपी, दस्ताने
  • आरामदायक ट्रेकिंग जूते
  • रेनकोट या पोंचो
  • पानी की बोतल, ग्लूकोज, ORS
  • धीरे-धीरे चढ़ाई करें
