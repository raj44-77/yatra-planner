Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 23, 2026

At Yatra, your privacy is extremely important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our website.

1. We Collect NO Personal Data

Yatra is a completely static website. We do not collect, store, or process any personal information. There is:

  • No sign-up or registration
  • No cookies (except essential browser defaults)
  • No tracking scripts or analytics
  • No data sent to any server
  • No forms that store data

2. Weather Data

Our weather page fetches data from Open-Meteo API — a free, open-source weather service. We do not send any user data to this API. Only the coordinates of Kedarnath are requested to fetch weather information.

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services that may have their own privacy policies:

  • Google Fonts — for displaying beautiful typography
  • Open-Meteo — for weather data (no API key, no tracking)

4. Local Storage

The Plan page may temporarily store your selected plan preferences (city, tier, number of travelers) in your browser's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is automatically cleared when you close your browser.

5. Children's Privacy

Yatra is safe for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children under 13.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

8. Cookies

Yatra itself does not use cookies. However, Google Fonts (used for displaying text) may set technical cookies required for font delivery. These cookies do not track you personally. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though fonts may not display correctly.

7. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please visit our Contact Page.
